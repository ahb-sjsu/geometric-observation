## Findings (all owed to the revision of the tex and the harness)

- **E1, error. Theorem 4(ii)'s equality clause** ("equality exactly on the symmetric family
  q₀ + q₁ = 1") is false in both directions.
  - Symmetric policies that miss: (0.8, 0.2) and (1, 0).
  - Non-symmetric policies that attain it: (0.3, 0.3) and (0, 0).
  - **Correct statement: equality iff q₁ ≥ q₀ and (q₀ + q₁ = 1 or q₀ = q₁).** I re-derived it.
    With u = q₀ + q₁ and v = q₁ − q₀, err = (1 − v)/2 and ρ = v²/(u(2 − u)). So equality holds
    iff v = √ρ, iff v ≥ 0 and (u = 1 or v = 0).
  - Lean `frontier_attained` proves only the "if" half, and only for ε ≤ ½.
  - The frontier e(ρ) = (1 − √ρ)/2 itself is unaffected.
- **G1, gap. Theorem 4(iii)'s five classes are asserted ("measured by 091"), not derived.**
  - "D(t*) = k" can fail to have a root, since D jumps at t = μ_i. The budget condition reads
    D(t*+) ≤ k ≤ D(t*−).
  - t* can be non-unique: in 091's P3, any t ∈ [1, 2.25] is optimal.
  - The "conceded" condition omits μ_i > t*.
  - *Fix:* write the t-representation. At fixed t, x = min(1, s²/(4λθμ)).
- **G2, gap (minor). Theorem 4(iv)** also needs the exchange lemma's two facts:
  - θ = 1 implies g ≥ γ;
  - θ = 0 implies g ≤ γ.
  
  Checked: they hold.
- **O1, overclaim (major). Theorem 6 resolves the v0.3 question only for k = 1.**
  - For k ≥ 2 the proof covers the additive axis-glance model, in which each glance is read
    separately and the leakage is Σθ_i R(e_i).
  - That is not the rank-k joint-subset game of Corollary 2, which the proof's own "open
    residue" calls open.
  - The surrogate is not the rank-k game either: non-axis glances would be stronger (4.88 vs
    3.50 at the 6-coordinate saddle).
  - The verifier found no joint-subset gap on two instances (V* − best = −7.9e-10 and −6.6e-12).
    This is evidence, not proof.
  - *Fix:* claim the resolution for k = 1 only. Define the surrogate, and call the rank-k case
    open, in the theorem, the title block and the open-questions summary.
- **O2, overclaim.** "(Or its binary twin)" in Theorem 6. The proof is LQG-only.
- **O3, overclaim (verification). S2's gate checks only inf ≤ V* + tol.** It would PASS if
  (≥) were false. Its "gap 1.4e-12" compares V* with a local-search value, which certifies
  nothing about (≥), and "−1.4e-10" came from a dev-seed run.
  - *Fix:* a one-sided gate (FAIL if any start finds J < V* − 1e-8), far random starts, and the
    name "falsification attempt".
- **O4, overclaim (coverage).** "All essential algebra … machine-checked" overstates the Lean.
  - Not formalized: the per-glance leakage formula, the numerator decrease, and the class
    assembly.
  - `junk_domination` is trivial and encodes neither unchanged quantity the paper cites it for.
  - S1–S3 do not net the water-level assembly; 091's `analytic_solve` builds the class rules
    in.
- **X1–X7, exposition.**
  - The module has 12 theorems, not 11.
  - Theorem 6's (≤) is the saddle policy against π_θ*, not "the minimax inequality".
  - A random single-coordinate glance with marginals θ* is impossible when Σθ = k > 1.
  - Theorem 4(iv): "unsaturated (θ_i < 1)".
  - Theorem 5 inherits the scope of Theorems 1–3, and gives a jointly diagonal optimum, not
    uniqueness. It also inherits VI-30's error in Theorem 3.
  - "Subadditivity" is unexplained; the natural inequality fails for redundant rows.
  - The gate figures are misquoted: thresholds 1e-10 to 1e-13; measured 8e-17 to 8e-15.
