    Chapter 21 — The Refresh Floor

    STUB [A] — draftable now. Source (observation-theory-campaigns): the sealed age-horizon sweep XPROTO-CSI-SWEEP2 (analysis/csi/PREREG-XPROTO-CSI-SWEEP2.md, sealed 2026-08-27, graded PASS); the superseded, unsealed exploration analysis/csi/CSI-refreshfloor.{json,png} (csi_sweep.py), whose fit its source records as refuted and keeps as record; the prediction results (csi_predict.py, CSI-PREDICT-NOTES.md); NTN (csi_ntn.py). House voice per Ch. 19.

    What this chapter must establish

    • The floor is not a convention, and it is not a fixed fraction of the coherence time. The maximum report/renew interval holding FC at target was first fitted, in an unsealed exploration on a real 5G NR physical layer (Sionna LDPC + TR38.901 fading), as $\phi \approx K\,T_{\mathrm{coh}}$ with $K \approx 0.177$, $R^2 \approx 0.92$. Its source records that fit as refuted: the floors were measured at a relaxed 0.15 BLER threshold against a 0.10 claim, and the fresh baseline (BLER $\approx 0.113$) never met the budget. The sealed rerun (XPROTO-CSI-SWEEP2) finds that at the 0.10 budget the largest compliant report period is 4/6/4 TTI at 10 Hz, 2/2/3 TTI at 25 Hz, and 1 TTI at 50 Hz and above (three seeds): the horizon collapses, and there is no proportionality law to fit. (The campaign repository called the refuted fit “the OT-14 law”; row OT-14 of this book’s ledger is a different test, the Third Crucible’s test of the revised principle P4, in which excess damage tracks drift and a refresh removes most of it.)
    • Prediction cannot buy horizon. The optimal linear (Wiener) predictor saturates within one coherence time; for a memoryless-past-$T_{\mathrm{coh}}$ Gaussian process this is information-theoretic, not an engineering limit [exploratory] (an unsealed exploration, CSI-PREDICT-NOTES.md). That wall sits well outside the sealed budget horizon above, so prediction is accurate over a window the budget has already closed. A wall, not a knob — the freshness dual of the rate–distortion converse.
    • The floor is consumer-relative too. A stricter target (URLLC 1e-3 vs eMBB 1e-1) shortens the floor; the same channel, two consumers, two floors.
    • When refreshing cannot close the loop. If already at the floor and still vacuous, the limit is a mechanism, not a cadence (deep-fade outage; NTN RTT $> T_{\mathrm{coh}}$, stale-on-arrival) — motivates the governor’s escalation (Ch. 23).

    Key figures / claims (→ ledger)

    • The sealed age horizon at the 0.10 budget against Doppler (XPROTO-CSI-SWEEP2); the refuted $\phi$-vs-$T_{\mathrm{coh}}$ fit (slope 0.177, $R^2$ 0.92) shown, if at all, as the kept record of a superseded exploration.
    • Predictor-saturation curve (naive vs Wiener, both saturate ≤ $T_{\mathrm{coh}}$) [exploratory].
    • NTN stale-on-arrival regime (RTT vs $T_{\mathrm{coh}}$).

    Boundary

    No proportionality constant survives at the true budget (XPROTO-CSI-SWEEP2), so none is carried to other substrates. Derive-then-measure, never fit-then-assert: the refuted fit is that rule’s own example. None of these results has a row in claims/LEDGER.md.
