## The honest accounting of what killed what

Of the four non-passes, **zero are clean theory refutations and four
are instrument deaths**: a family without interior (OT-12), a bar
contradicting the program's own record (OT-10), a degenerate estimator
(OT-9), a knob without a gradient (OT-11). The theory-relevant signals
inside them cut both ways — OT-10's theorem quantities all held at 5%
precision; OT-12's ungraded band and OT-11's 4%-vs-30% reduction lean
adverse to the v0.2 revisions. None of it is verdict-grade, and none
of it may be graded retroactively.

## The campaign-level finding, stated once

The First Crucible went 5-for-7 with instrument seals that iterated
(three of its tests burned one or two seals before grading cleanly).
The Second went 1-for-5 with appendices authored and declared
final-revision in a single sitting. **Sealing discipline has a rate
limit: tests authored in bulk die of their instruments, and
final-revision clauses convert instrument defects into permanent
verdicts.** The program's own standing rule ("before believing 'X
holds along Y', check Y varies") was violated three times in one day —
by the author who wrote the rule down that morning. Encode it: from
here, **no appendix may be sealed on the day its family is first
constructed** — a family must show its interior (knob sweep, ESS,
variance floors) in a committed shakedown before bars bind to it. This
is the campaign's durable product, alongside OT-8.
