\begin{abstract}
GO-11 closed the static consumer-relative rate--work region: the $m=1$
theory in full, the $m=2$ frontier as a matrix water-level system, and
the binary pair in closed form. Every source there was memoryless and
every context simultaneous. This problem statement opens the dynamic
extension: jointly stationary source--context \emph{processes}, a
context that \emph{ages}, and per-symbol rate--work coordinates. The
organizing guess is that the water level takes its third promotion ---
from scalars ($m=1$), to $2\times2$ matrices ($m=2$), to \emph{spectra}
(processes) --- with the GO-8 staleness law emerging as a limit. Nothing
in this document is a claim; the conjectures are gates for future
registrations, and the novelty obligations are listed.
\end{abstract}

\section{The problem}

Let $(Y_t,V_t)_{t\in\mathbb Z}$ be a jointly stationary Gaussian pair
process with spectral density matrix
$\mathbf S(\omega)=\begin{pmatrix}S_Y&S_{YV}\\S_{YV}^*&S_V\end{pmatrix}$,
coherence $\kappa(\omega)=|S_{YV}|^2/(S_YS_V)$. The eraser's side
information is the \emph{aged, noisy} context
\[
S_t\;=\;V_{t-\Delta}+U_t,\qquad U_t\ \text{white},\ \mathrm{Var}\,U=
\tau^2,\qquad\Delta\ge0\ \text{the staleness},
\]
observed as the full process $S^n$. A block code produces the
reproduction process $\hat Y^n$ with per-symbol Hamming--free (MSE)
distortion $\tfrac1n\sum E(Y_t-\hat Y_t)^2\le D$, and the coordinates of
interest are the per-symbol limits
\[
R(D)=\lim_n\tfrac1n I(T^n;\hat Y^n),\qquad
L(D,\Delta)=\lim_n\tfrac1n I(T^n;\hat Y^n\mid S^n),
\]
with $kT\ln2\cdot L$ the ideal per-symbol reset work, exactly as in
GO-10/GO-11 but per unit time. Consumers never see $S$; Markov
$\hat Y^n-X^n-S^n$ throughout.

\section{Anchors already in the ledger}

(i) $\Delta$-degeneracy: for memoryless pairs and $\Delta=0$ this is
GO-11's static theory (Theorems 1--10 there). (ii) The GO-8 staleness
law: decode thresholds track an age-dependent discount on AR(1) sources
--- measured, $[$replicated$]$ with the 053 caveat --- but GO-8 had no
exact region behind it; GO-12 is where that region would live. (iii) The
work side at $\Delta\to\infty$ must collapse to the marginal
rate--distortion function of the $Y$ process (the context decorrelates),
and at $\tau^2\to\infty$ likewise.

\section{Conjectures}

\begin{conjecture}[Spectral CR function; the third promotion]
\label{conj:spectral}
For the rank-one read of the pair process,
\[
L(D,\Delta)\;=\;\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}\tfrac12\log_2
g^\star\!\bigl(\omega;\,D(\omega)\bigr)\,\tfrac{d\omega}{2\pi},
\]
where $g^\star(\omega;\cdot)$ is GO-11's static quadratic-root CR
function applied per frequency with effective parameters
$\rho^2(\omega)=\kappa(\omega)$ (staleness entering only through the
lag-$\Delta$ phase and the noise floor: the conditional spectrum
$S_{Y\mid S}(\omega)=S_Y(1-\kappa\,\tfrac{S_V}{S_V+\tau^2})$ is
$\Delta$-free for linear aging of this form --- the nontrivial content
is which \emph{aging models} break this), and $D(\omega)$ is a
distortion allocation determined by \emph{two coupled spectral water
levels} $\gamma_0(\omega),\gamma_1(\omega)$ solving the weighted
stationarity system of GO-11's Theorem~3 per frequency under a common
pair of Lagrange prices. The $m=2$ process region takes the same form
with the matrix water levels of GO-11's Theorem~9 per frequency.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{fact}[$\Delta$-invariance under path access; settled and netted,
GO-P-2026-065]\label{lem:recoding}
In the circulant embedding, with $P$ the cyclic shift and
$S^{(\Delta)}=P^\Delta V+U$: the joint laws satisfy the \emph{recoding
identity} $(Y,V,S^{(\Delta)})\overset d=(Y,V,P^\Delta S^{(0)})$
(all three cross-covariances match because $P$ commutes with every
circulant matrix, e.g.\ $\mathrm{Cov}(Y,S^{(\Delta)})=\rho C_VP^{-\Delta}$,
and $P^\Delta(C_V+\tau^2I)P^{-\Delta}=C_V+\tau^2I$); since $P^\Delta$ is
a deterministic bijection, $\sigma(S^{(\Delta)})$ carries the same
information as $\sigma(S^{(0)})$, so \emph{every conditional functional
that depends on $S$ only through $\sigma(S)$} --- conditional mutual
information, the conditional rate--distortion coordinate $L(D)$,
conditional entropy, MMSE --- is exactly $\Delta$-invariant at every
finite $n$. (Note $\Sigma_{Y\mid S}$-invariance alone is weaker: the
conditional-mean \emph{map} does depend on $\Delta$, so label-aligned
functionals like $\mathbb E[Y_tS_t]$ are not invariant; the recoding
identity is the airtight form.) Finite Toeplitz windows deviate only
through edge terms, $O(1/n)$ at fixed $\Delta$ (empirically $\approx$
linear in $\Delta$ over the probed range). Governed harness
GO-P-2026-065: circulant invariance to $3.3\times10^{-16}$, edge ratio
$0.5000000000$ under $n$-doubling.
\end{fact}

\begin{fact}[Slice access: the exact staleness tax, with encoder access
width; settled and netted, GO-P-2026-065]\label{lem:slice}
If the eraser instead sees only the time-local context sample
$S=V_{t\pm\Delta}+U$ (GO-8's situation), the single-record conditional
coordinate is GO-11's static quadratic-root CR function with the
substitution determined by the \emph{encoder's} access:
(B) records built from $(Y_t,V_t)$ alone --- per-symbol codebooks ---
pay the quadratic at $\rho$ unchanged, $s\to s/a^{2\Delta}$
(write $S=a^\Delta V_t+(W+U)$, $W\perp(Y_t,V_t)$, rescale); (A) records
granted the context-epoch latent $V_{t\pm\Delta}$ (equivalently, by
pair sufficiency, any path encoder with a single-symbol target) pay the
strictly smaller value at $\rho\to\rho a^\Delta$, $\tau^2$ unchanged
--- the gap reaches $0.053$ bits on the probe grid, so the tax has an
encoder-side access-width component as well. Both are strictly
increasing in $\Delta$ ($P(1)<0$, $P(1/D)>0$ bracket the root;
$dg^\star/d\rho^2<0$, $dg^\star/ds>0$) with common limit
$\tfrac12\log_2(1/D)$. An R-IND-5 pass refuted the first draft of this
fact (it asserted the (A) formula for (B) records); the corrected
pairing is what the governed harness nets, both substitutions against
their own direct channel programs to $\le2.4\times10^{-13}$.
\end{fact}

\begin{remark}
Facts~\ref{lem:recoding}--\ref{lem:slice} settle the causal-origin
question the cheap way: the staleness tax is an \emph{access-width}
phenomenon --- at the eraser (path vs slice) and at the encoder
(context-epoch latent vs time-$t$ variables) --- not a delay
phenomenon. GO-8's measured age-dependence is the operational face of
the slice regime.
\end{remark}

\begin{conjecture}[The causal-path eraser interpolates]
\label{conj:causal}
Fact~\ref{lem:recoding} (full path: zero tax) and
Fact~\ref{lem:slice} (single slice: the exact static tax) are the two
ends. For the \emph{causal-path} eraser (at time $t$ it sees
$S_{\le t}$, $S_t=V_{t-\Delta}+U_t$), the per-symbol conditional
coordinate lies strictly between, is strictly increasing in $\Delta$
for $a\in(0,1)$, and approaches the slice value as the context process
decorrelates; its exact form (plausibly a Kalman/innovations
water-filling) is the open object of this rung.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[Spectral tilt]\label{conj:tilt}
The encoder-side tilt (GO-11, 061/062) becomes spectral: the $L$-optimal
record concentrates its precision in frequency bands where the context
coherence $\kappa(\omega)\tfrac{S_V}{S_V+\tau^2}$ is largest --- records
written in the bands the eraser's context can read --- with an
analytically-zero control in any band of zero coherence.
\end{conjecture}

successor) already contain Conjecture~\ref{conj:spectral}'s noncausal
form? Novelty sweep obligation: conditional/two-sided RD for processes,
remote-source RD with delayed side information, causal RD
(Neuhoff--Gilbert lineage), zero-delay work. No claim before the sweep.
\end{question}

numerics?
\end{question}

\begin{question}
What is the right operational face? Candidate: 058-lineage decode
thresholds on materialized codebooks for AR(1) pairs at three staleness
values, with the noncausal-zero control of
Fact~\ref{lem:recoding} as the physics gate separating path from slice
erasure, and the (A)-vs-(B) encoder gap of Fact~\ref{lem:slice} as a
second measurable face.
\end{question}
