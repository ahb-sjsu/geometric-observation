\begin{abstract}
GO-11 closed the static consumer-relative rate--work region: the $m=1$
theory in full, the $m=2$ frontier as a matrix water-level system, and
the binary pair in closed form. Every source there was memoryless and
every context simultaneous. This problem statement opens the dynamic
extension: jointly stationary source--context \emph{processes}, a
context that \emph{ages}, and per-symbol rate--work coordinates. The
organizing guess is that the water level takes its third promotion ---
from scalars ($m=1$), to $2\times2$ matrices ($m=2$), to \emph{spectra}
(processes) --- with the GO-8 staleness law emerging as a limit. Nothing
in this document is a claim; the conjectures are gates for future
registrations, and the novelty obligations are listed.
\end{abstract}

\section{The problem}

Let $(Y_t,V_t)_{t\in\mathbb Z}$ be a jointly stationary Gaussian pair
process with spectral density matrix
$\mathbf S(\omega)=\begin{pmatrix}S_Y&S_{YV}\\S_{YV}^*&S_V\end{pmatrix}$,
coherence $\kappa(\omega)=|S_{YV}|^2/(S_YS_V)$. The eraser's side
information is the \emph{aged, noisy} context
\[
S_t\;=\;V_{t-\Delta}+U_t,\qquad U_t\ \text{white},\ \mathrm{Var}\,U=
\tau^2,\qquad\Delta\ge0\ \text{the staleness},
\]
observed as the full process $S^n$. A block code produces the
reproduction process $\hat Y^n$ with per-symbol Hamming--free (MSE)
distortion $\tfrac1n\sum E(Y_t-\hat Y_t)^2\le D$, and the coordinates of
interest are the per-symbol limits
\[
R(D)=\lim_n\tfrac1n I(T^n;\hat Y^n),\qquad
L(D,\Delta)=\lim_n\tfrac1n I(T^n;\hat Y^n\mid S^n),
\]
with $kT\ln2\cdot L$ the ideal per-symbol reset work, exactly as in
GO-10/GO-11 but per unit time. Consumers never see $S$; Markov
$\hat Y^n-X^n-S^n$ throughout.

\section{Anchors already in the ledger}

(i) $\Delta$-degeneracy: for memoryless pairs and $\Delta=0$ this is
GO-11's static theory (Theorems 1--10 there). (ii) The GO-8 staleness
law: decode thresholds track an age-dependent discount on AR(1) sources
--- measured, $[$replicated$]$ with the 053 caveat --- but GO-8 had no
exact region behind it; GO-12 is where that region would live. (iii) The
work side at $\Delta\to\infty$ must collapse to the marginal
rate--distortion function of the $Y$ process (the context decorrelates),
and at $\tau^2\to\infty$ likewise.

\section{Conjectures}

\begin{conjecture}[Spectral CR function; the third promotion]
\label{conj:spectral}
For the rank-one read of the pair process,
\[
L(D,\Delta)\;=\;\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}\tfrac12\log_2
g^\star\!\bigl(\omega;\,D(\omega)\bigr)\,\tfrac{d\omega}{2\pi},
\]
where $g^\star(\omega;\cdot)$ is GO-11's static quadratic-root CR
function applied per frequency with effective parameters
$\rho^2(\omega)=\kappa(\omega)$ (staleness entering only through the
lag-$\Delta$ phase and the noise floor: the conditional spectrum
$S_{Y\mid S}(\omega)=S_Y(1-\kappa\,\tfrac{S_V}{S_V+\tau^2})$ is
$\Delta$-free for linear aging of this form --- the nontrivial content
is which \emph{aging models} break this), and $D(\omega)$ is a
distortion allocation determined by \emph{two coupled spectral water
levels} $\gamma_0(\omega),\gamma_1(\omega)$ solving the weighted
stationarity system of GO-11's Theorem~3 per frequency under a common
pair of Lagrange prices. The $m=2$ process region takes the same form
with the matrix water levels of GO-11's Theorem~9 per frequency.

\emph{Attribution} (novelty sweep, 2026-08-05; Gray's 1972 report
page-verified in full): the functional $L$ coincides at the
single-letter level with \emph{Gray's conditional rate--distortion
function} \cite{gray72,gray73}, whose operational meaning is side
information at both encoder and decoder; existence of the per-symbol
limit for stationary-ergodic pairs is Leiner--Gray \cite{leinergray}.
The scalar Gaussian evaluation at conditional variance is
\cite[Thm.~7]{gray72}; vector-Gaussian conditional/Wyner--Ziv
water-filling with a \emph{single} water level over the conditional
covariance's eigenvalues is \cite{gkagkos,stylianou}; single-level
spectral water-filling for remote sources is \cite{kipnis}. Our
allocation structure generalizes Gray's equal-slope decomposition
\cite[Thm.~5]{gray72} from a discrete conditioning alphabet to the
frequency domain. Not found in the sweep: the spectral conditional RDF
for jointly stationary Gaussian pairs, the two coupled water levels,
the third-party (neither-encoder-nor-consumer) conditioning semantics,
or the delayed-noisy side process. Both mandated library pulls are now
resolved DOES-NOT-CONTAIN at high confidence (2026-08-05, record in
the novelty file): Pinsker's book contains no fidelity-criterion
quantity of any kind (whole-book term-absence tests over the scan's
OCR index; \S10.5's ``entropy of one process w.r.t.\ another'' is a
KL rate --- a terminology trap now on record), and Gray 1973's own
companion report SEL-72-047, read in full, confines the Gaussian
content to the scalar pair, cross-referenced as ``the examples of
[8]''. Residual obligations before final claim wording: an
institutional pull of the T-IT 19(4) text itself (minutes, via the
venue package), and the same page-level treatment of Leiner--Gray
1974 \cite{leinergray} plus the Wyner 1976--78 Gaussian-SI line ---
the natural next places a spectral conditional formula could first
appear.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{fact}[$\Delta$-invariance under path access; settled and netted,
GO-P-2026-065]\label{lem:recoding}
In the circulant embedding, with $P$ the cyclic shift and
$S^{(\Delta)}=P^\Delta V+U$: the joint laws satisfy the \emph{recoding
identity} $(Y,V,S^{(\Delta)})\overset d=(Y,V,P^\Delta S^{(0)})$
(all three cross-covariances match because $P$ commutes with every
circulant matrix, e.g.\ $\mathrm{Cov}(Y,S^{(\Delta)})=\rho C_VP^{-\Delta}$,
and $P^\Delta(C_V+\tau^2I)P^{-\Delta}=C_V+\tau^2I$); since $P^\Delta$ is
a deterministic bijection, $\sigma(S^{(\Delta)})$ carries the same
information as $\sigma(S^{(0)})$, so \emph{every conditional functional
that depends on $S$ only through $\sigma(S)$} --- conditional mutual
information, the conditional rate--distortion coordinate $L(D)$,
conditional entropy, MMSE --- is exactly $\Delta$-invariant at every
finite $n$. (Note $\Sigma_{Y\mid S}$-invariance alone is weaker: the
conditional-mean \emph{map} does depend on $\Delta$, so label-aligned
functionals like $\mathbb E[Y_tS_t]$ are not invariant; the recoding
identity is the airtight form.) Finite Toeplitz windows deviate only
through edge terms, $O(1/n)$ at fixed $\Delta$ (empirically $\approx$
linear in $\Delta$ over the probed range). Governed harness
GO-P-2026-065: circulant invariance to $3.3\times10^{-16}$, edge ratio
$0.5000000000$ under $n$-doubling.

\emph{Attribution} (novelty sweeps, 2026-08-05): invariance of
conditional functionals under bijective recoding of the conditioning
variable is classical folklore, and circulant--Toeplitz $O(1/n)$
asymptotics are standard \cite{grayToeplitz}. The rate-level
\emph{phenomenon} is also in print for memoryless pairs: Simeone and
Permuter \cite[Lemma~1]{simeonepermuter} note that noncausal
availability makes zero-delay conditional RD delay-proof (while
positive causal delay makes memoryless side information useless), and
Matsuta and Uyematsu \cite{matsuta} characterize when unknown delay
leaves the Wyner--Ziv function unchanged. Ours is only the packaged
finite-$n$ recoding identity, for a conditioner that is neither encoder
nor decoder.
\end{fact}

\begin{fact}[Slice access: the exact staleness tax, with encoder access
width; settled and netted, GO-P-2026-065]\label{lem:slice}
If the eraser instead sees only the time-local context sample
$S=V_{t\pm\Delta}+U$ (GO-8's situation), the single-record conditional
coordinate is GO-11's static quadratic-root CR function with the
substitution determined by the \emph{encoder's} access:
(B) records built from $(Y_t,V_t)$ alone --- per-symbol codebooks ---
pay the quadratic at $\rho$ unchanged, $s\to s/a^{2\Delta}$
(write $S=a^\Delta V_t+(W+U)$, $W\perp(Y_t,V_t)$, rescale); (A) records
granted the context-epoch latent $V_{t\pm\Delta}$ (equivalently, by
pair sufficiency, any path encoder with a single-symbol target) pay the
strictly smaller value at $\rho\to\rho a^\Delta$, $\tau^2$ unchanged
--- the gap reaches $0.053$ bits on the probe grid, so the tax has an
encoder-side access-width component as well. Both are strictly
increasing in $\Delta$ ($P(1)<0$, $P(1/D)>0$ bracket the root;
$dg^\star/d\rho^2<0$, $dg^\star/ds>0$) with common limit
$\tfrac12\log_2(1/D)$. An R-IND-5 pass refuted the first draft of this
fact (it asserted the (A) formula for (B) records); the corrected
pairing is what the governed harness nets, both substitutions against
their own direct channel programs to $\le2.4\times10^{-13}$.

\emph{Attribution} (novelty sweeps, 2026-08-05; mandatory rescope): the
substitution \emph{device} --- Markov decay absorbed into the side
channel --- is published. Simeone and Permuter
\cite[Eq.~(49), test channel Eq.~(67)]{simeonepermuter} obtain
$R_d(D)=\tfrac12\log_2\!\bigl((1-\rho^{2d}+\sigma_N^2)/D\bigr)$ for a
hidden Gauss--Markov source with noiselessly delayed decoder side
information --- our $s\to s/a^{2\Delta}$ in a different normalization
--- and Sun and Cyr \cite[Eq.~(7)]{suncyr} give the conditional-MI
staleness form $-\tfrac12\log_2(1-a^{2\Delta})$ for AR(1) in the
age-of-information literature. In the jointly Gaussian block setting,
Wyner--Ziv no-rate-loss additionally makes encoder-side availability of
$S$ rate-irrelevant \cite{wynerziv}. What is new here is confined to:
the noisy delayed slice held by a \emph{third-party} conditioner, and
the \emph{encoder-access dichotomy} --- whether $a^\Delta$ enters the
read correlation or the effective side-information noise is decided by
which variable the record may depend on, which none of the found
sources states.
\end{fact}

\begin{remark}
Facts~\ref{lem:recoding}--\ref{lem:slice} settle the causal-origin
question the cheap way: the staleness tax is an \emph{access-width}
phenomenon --- at the eraser (path vs slice) and at the encoder
(context-epoch latent vs time-$t$ variables) --- not a delay
phenomenon. GO-8's measured age-dependence is the operational face of
the slice regime. The two nearest prior statements sit in opposite
corners and are both attributed: for \emph{noiseless} Markov sampling
the causal path collapses to the freshest sample
\cite[Lemma~1]{suncyr} (width collapses), and for \emph{memoryless}
pairs noncausal access collapses delay
\cite[Lemma~1]{simeonepermuter}; the dissociation itself --- exact zero
under path access versus a strictly increasing closed-form tax under
slice access, on the same source --- is not found in the swept
literature.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Physics scoping]
On the thermodynamic side the framing is hemmed by known work, all of
which we inherit rather than claim: static conditional erasure work is
set by the conditional entropy given a reference (del Rio et al.
\cite{delrio}; Sagawa--Ueda \cite{sagawaueda}); that retaining stale,
non-predictive information is thermodynamically penalized is the
``nostalgia'' of Still, Sivak, Bell and Crooks \cite{still}; and
delay-degraded \emph{work extraction} is quantified in the
time-delayed-feedback-demon literature \cite{rosinberg}. Our claim is
confined to the erasure side of the ledger: the exact dynamic
conditional-Landauer curve $W(\Delta)$ for resetting a record against a
reference decorrelated by $a^\Delta$, which the sweeps did not find
written down. (No co-occurrence of age-of-information and Landauer
erasure was found at the arXiv-metadata level as of August 2026; that
bridge claim carries a metadata-only caveat.)
\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[The conditional-variance reduction; the access family
settled in the single-record setting]\label{thm:qred}
Let $V$ be the stationary AR(1) context (pole $a$, unit variance),
$Y_t=\rho V_t+N_t$ (unit variance), observations $S_s=V_s+U_s$,
$\mathrm{Var}\,U=\tau^2>0$, and let the record be single-letter (built
from $(Y_t,V_t)$; the (B) scope of Fact~\ref{lem:slice}), with
$D\in(0,1)$ (the active regime). Then for every context
$\sigma$-algebra $\mathcal G$ generated by a \emph{nonempty} subset of
the observations $\{S_s\}$ --- more generally, any $\mathcal G$ jointly
Gaussian with the $V$-process and independent of the consumer and
record noises, with $q_{\mathcal G}<1$; these hypotheses are
load-bearing, since a $\mathcal G$ correlated with $N_t$ breaks the
sufficiency step ---
\[
L_{\mathcal G}(D)\;=\;\tfrac12\log_2
g^\star\!\Bigl(\rho^2,\ \tfrac1{1-q_{\mathcal G}},\ D\Bigr),
\qquad q_{\mathcal G}=\mathrm{Var}\bigl(V_t\mid\mathcal G\bigr),
\]
with $g^\star(\rho^2,s,D)$ the static quadratic root of GO-11's
Theorem~2: the entire access class enters through one number, the
conditional variance of the context at the record epoch.
[Proof: $\hat V=\mathbb E[V_t\mid\mathcal G]$ is a sufficient statistic
for $(Y_t,V_t,\hat{\mathbf Y})$ within $\mathcal G$; rescaling
$\hat V/(1-q)$ gives an effective side channel $V_t+e'$ with
$e'\perp(V_t,N_t)$ jointly Gaussian and
$\mathrm{Var}\,e'=q/(1-q)$, i.e.\ the static normal form at
$s_{\mathrm{eff}}=1/(1-q)$; the sufficiency step is channel-agnostic
--- $(T,\hat{\mathbf Y})\perp\!\!\!\perp\mathcal G\mid\hat V$ holds for
\emph{every} randomized record, so the reduction maps the full problem
onto the full static problem and the record-class optimality is
\emph{imported} from the static theorem's converse, not re-proved
here. At $q_{\mathcal G}=1$ (empty access) the continuous extension is
$L=\tfrac12\log_2(1/D)$.]
Consequently, for the nested access classes at equal lag $\Delta$ ---
slice $\{S_{t+\Delta}\}$ $\subset$ causal prefix $\{S_{\le t+\Delta}\}$
$\subset$ full path --- the coordinates strictly interpolate,
\[
L_{\mathrm{slice}}(D,\Delta)\;>\;L_{\mathrm{prefix}}(D,\Delta)\;>\;
L_{\mathrm{path}}(D)\qquad(\tau^2>0,\ a\in(0,1),\ \text{active regime}),
\]
with closed/computable forms: $q_{\mathrm{slice}}=1-a^{2\Delta}/(1+\tau^2)$
(recovering Fact~\ref{lem:slice}(B) exactly, $s_{\mathrm{eff}}
=(1+\tau^2)/a^{2\Delta}$); $q_{\mathrm{prefix}}$ the scalar
steady-state Kalman fixed-lag-smoother variance (Riccati fixed point
plus a finite geometric improvement recursion, converging to
$q_{\mathrm{path}}$ geometrically in $\Delta$); and $q_{\mathrm{path}}
=\int S_V\tau^2/(S_V+\tau^2)\,\tfrac{d\omega}{2\pi}$, the noncausal
Wiener MMSE. Strictness follows from strict variance reduction under
the nesting and $dg^\star\!/ds>0$, and is exact at every finite
$\Delta$: the prefix--path gap equals
$C^{2\Delta}(P_f-q_{\mathrm{path}})$ exactly, with
$C=aP_f/P_{\mathrm{pred}}\in(0,1)$ --- never zero, though it falls
below double-precision resolution beyond
$\Delta\approx\log\varepsilon/\log C^2$. The hypothesis $a\in(0,1)$ is
load-bearing: at $a=0$ the classes coincide at $\Delta=0$ and the
slice is vacuous for $\Delta\ge1$. In the thermodynamic reading this
is the exact dynamic conditional-Landauer curve: $W(\Delta)=kT\ln2\cdot
L_{\mathrm{prefix}}(D,\Delta)$ falls geometrically from the
filtering-set cost to the smoothing-set floor as the eraser is allowed
to wait. [Pre-assertion numerics, seed 20260916: reduction vs direct
channel optimization to $1.4\times10^{-13}$ on four access classes;
Riccati and Wiener forms vs windowed covariances to
$2\times10^{-16}$; interpolation strict at lags $0,1,3,6$ and at a
second instance.]
\end{theorem}

\begin{conjecture}[The process-rate causal object; the open residue]
\label{conj:causal}
Theorem~\ref{thm:qred} settles the causal-prefix eraser in the
\emph{single-record} setting, where the Kalman filtering-vs-smoothing
gap carries the interpolation --- as the sweep's honesty caveat
anticipated. What remains open is the \emph{process-rate} version:
block records amortized across symbols, with the per-symbol coordinate
defined via Markov-constrained causal conditioning in the sense of
Lev--Khina \cite{levkhina} --- a deliberate choice, since the
Kostina--Hassibi causally-conditioned-DI definition \cite{kostina}
provably collapses to the full-path endpoint in Gaussian settings. The
nearest known object (causal decoder side information, undelayed) is
explicitly open even there \cite{levkhina}; the vector NRDF
water-filling caution \cite{stavrou} applies; and the noiseless
endpoint collapses by \cite[Lemma~1]{suncyr}, so any interpolation
theorem must be carried by $\tau^2>0$.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[Spectral tilt]\label{conj:tilt}
The encoder-side tilt (GO-11, 061/062) becomes spectral: the $L$-optimal
record concentrates its precision in frequency bands where the context
coherence $\kappa(\omega)\tfrac{S_V}{S_V+\tau^2}$ is largest --- records
written in the bands the eraser's context can read --- with an
analytically-zero control in any band of zero coherence.
\end{conjecture}

\section{Questions}

\begin{question}
Does Gray's conditional-RD theory for stationary sources (or any
successor) already contain Conjecture~\ref{conj:spectral}'s noncausal
form? Novelty sweep obligation: conditional/two-sided RD for processes,
remote-source RD with delayed side information, causal RD
(Neuhoff--Gilbert lineage), zero-delay work. No claim before the sweep.
\end{question}

\begin{question}
Is there a process analog of Theorem 10 (binary pair process with
Markov flips) in closed form, or does the discrete side stop at
numerics?
\end{question}

\begin{question}
What is the right operational face? Candidate: 058-lineage decode
thresholds on materialized codebooks for AR(1) pairs at three staleness
values, with the noncausal-zero control of
Fact~\ref{lem:recoding} as the physics gate separating path from slice
erasure, and the (A)-vs-(B) encoder gap of Fact~\ref{lem:slice} as a
second measurable face.
\end{question}
