\begin{abstract}
GO-12 settled the single-record causal-prefix eraser (the Kalman
family); the process-rate object --- block records erased cell by
cell, each against only the causal prefix of an aging reference ---
remains open, and its nearest published neighbor (Gauss--Markov
tracking with causal decoder side information, Lev--Khina 2020)
is explicitly open even without delay. This statement promotes it
from GO-12 residue to its own campaign. Nothing here is a claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{The problem}
Sources and observations as in GO-12 ($Y=\rho V+N$, $V$ AR(1),
$S_s=V_s+U_s$). A block record $\hat Y^n$ is produced noncausally by
the encoder; the eraser resets cell $t$ at time $t+\Delta$ holding
only $\sigma(S_{\le t+\Delta})$ (and, definitional fork, possibly the
already-erased cells). The process-rate coordinate is a per-symbol
limit of summed per-cell conditional informations.

\begin{question}[The definitional fork --- part of the problem]
Candidate coordinates: (a) Markov-constrained causal conditioning in
the sense of Lev--Khina, $\tfrac1n\sum_tI(T^n;\hat Y_t\mid
S^{t+\Delta},\hat Y^{t-1})$; (b) the same without record memory;
(c) Kostina--Hassibi causally-conditioned DI --- \emph{known} to
collapse to the full-path endpoint in Gaussian settings, so the
interpolation is well-posed only under (a)/(b). Which definition is
the thermodynamically operational one (a reset protocol realizes it)
is itself a question the campaign must settle first.
\end{question}

\begin{conjecture}[Strict interpolation with an innovations form]
Under the operational definition, the process-rate causal coordinate
lies strictly between GO-12's slice and path endpoints for
$\tau^2>0$, is strictly increasing in $\Delta$, and admits a
Kalman/innovations characterization --- with the caution that vector
NRDF water-filling closed forms were refuted in general
(Stavrou--Tanaka--Tatikonda), so only scalar closed forms should be
expected cheaply.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{question}
The honesty caveat inherited from the GO-12 sweep: does the object
reduce to the classical filtering-vs-smoothing gap plus bookkeeping,
or does record memory ($\hat Y^{t-1}$ in the conditioning) create
genuinely new structure? The first probe is numerical: compute
(a)/(b) at small $n$ against the 066 endpoints.
\end{question}
