\begin{abstract}
GO-14's verification pass discovered that its own U-independence
hypothesis fences off a different object: records permitted to
correlate with the noise of the eraser's aging reference erase at a
fraction of the reference-independent cost (probe: $0.079$ vs
$0.567$ bits/symbol at $\Delta=0$, $n=16$, $D=0.3$ --- an $86\%$
collapse). This statement promotes that object to its own campaign.
The probe's operational verdict frames it: the collapse is gated by
\emph{encoder lookahead past the eraser's access horizon}, and the
realistic coordinate is the $S$-constructible family (the record
writer reads the aging context), with general $U$-access a labeled
fictitious relaxation. Nothing here is a claim; every probe number
is a class-conditional upper bound with no lower-bound face yet.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}
As in GO-14: $V$ AR(1), pole $a$; $Y=\rho V+N$; reference
$S=V+U$, $\operatorname{Var}(U)=\tau^2$; per-cell erasure of
$\hat Y^n$ against $(S^{t+\Delta},\hat Y^{t-1})$; coordinate
$L_a(\Delta)$. Families: $\mathcal F_0$ (U-independent, GO-14's),
$\mathcal F_{\Delta c}$ ($\Delta$-lag-causal $U$-coupling,
$A_u[t,s]=0$ for $s>t+\Delta$ --- the chain-rule-preserving class),
$\mathcal F_S$ ($\hat Y=A_yY+A_sS+Z$: the encoder reads the
reference, with an explicit lookahead-window parameter), and
$\mathcal F_U$ (general $U$-access --- fictitious: it resolves $S$
into $V+U$).

\begin{proposition}[Corrected identity --- from the GO-14 R-IND-5
record, re-verified at 24 probe optimizers]
$nL_a=\mathrm{block}+C_\Delta-\sum_t
I(S_{>t+\Delta};\hat Y_t\mid T,S^{t+\Delta},\hat Y^{t-1})$; the
correction vanishes iff the record is $\Delta$-lag causally
$U$-coupled. At probe optimizers of $\mathcal F_{\Delta c}$ the
correction is $0$ to $10^{-16}$; at the $\mathcal F_U$ winner it
carries $74\times$ the final value (only the difference of the
diverging terms is physical near the deterministic-record boundary).
\end{proposition}

\begin{conjecture}[Strict lookahead hierarchy]
For all $n\ge8$, $\Delta\le n-2$, and any noise floor
$\sigma_0^2\le10^{-2}$:
$\min_{\mathcal F_U}<\min_{\mathcal F_S}<\min_{\mathcal F_{\Delta
c}}<\min_{\mathcal F_0}$, with the $\Delta$-causal optimum
essentially causally-$S$-constructible ($A_u\approx A_v$ measured)
--- within-horizon $U$-coupling adds nothing beyond accessible $S$.
Probe anchors at $(16,0)$: $0.079<0.290<0.522<0.567$.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[Horizon-matched anti-coupling]
Optimizers concentrate $\ge60\%$ of their $U$-coupling mass,
negatively, on the single first-beyond-horizon band $s=t+\Delta+1$
(measured $63$--$93\%$ across all probe winners;
$\operatorname{corr}(\hat Y_t,U_{t+1})\approx-0.245$): the record
\emph{anticipates the first reference sample the eraser cannot
see} --- GO-14's horizon-matched mechanism transplanted from
$V$-cancellation to $U$-anticipation, and a cross-cell code effect
(a lone band on a scalar record recovers almost none of it).
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[$\Delta$-reversal]
$\min_{\mathcal F_U}L_a(\Delta)$ is strictly increasing in $\Delta$,
rejoining the block coordinate at $\Delta=n$ --- the lag knob
reverses sign across the $U$-coupling boundary (the
$\mathcal F_0$ coordinate strictly decreases; probe: $0.079\to
0.201\to0.219$ vs $0.567\to0.536\to0.535$ at $n=16$). The
$\mathcal F_S$ shape is open.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[Boundary attainment and the floor law]
At $\sigma_0=0$ the infimum over $\mathcal F_U$ is attained only on
the singular (deterministic-record) closure; $\sigma_0\mapsto\min
L_a$ is continuous and increasing with the floor constraint active
(probe: $0.079/0.105/0.149/0.366$ at
$\sigma_0^2=10^{-8}/10^{-6}/10^{-4}/10^{-2}$, all below
$\mathrm{block}_{16}=0.535$ --- the phenomenon survives realistic
noise; the \emph{value} is floor-relative and every claim must
carry its floor). The $\sigma_0\to0$ limit at fixed $n$ is open.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{question}[The lower-bound face --- the campaign gate]
Extend Theorem R to the extended moment coordinates
$(H_w,H_u,\Gamma)$: the denominator leg is already convex
(inf-of-affine pivots, monotone composition); the numerator
regrouping is the prover target. Until it lands, every number above
is a class-conditional upper bound and no $n$-trend may be claimed
(probe $n$-scan variance is optimization-depth, not physics).
\end{question}

\begin{question}[The Landauer face]
The erasure cost of a record against a causally-growing reference
is \emph{not encoder-independent}: it is gated by how far past the
eraser's horizon the record-writer could read. What reset protocol
realizes the lookahead discount, and what does the discount cost at
write time? (The GO-9 coordinated-reset lane is the in-house
exterior.)
\end{question}
