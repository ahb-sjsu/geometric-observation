\begin{abstract}
GO-10 measured the complementarity tax two consumers pay on one static
source; GO-11 solved the exact region behind it, through the $m=2$
frontier system with its $2\times2$ matrix water levels; GO-12 showed
the staleness of the eraser's context is an access-width phenomenon,
reduced entirely to one conditional variance $q_{\mathcal G}$. GO-13
asks the merged question: \emph{what do two consumers pay when the
eraser's context ages?} Nothing here is a claim; the conjectures are
gates for future registrations.
\end{abstract}

\section{The problem}

Take GO-11's $m=2$ setup verbatim (whitened latent $Z\in\mathbb
R^{2+r}$, reads $y_A,y_B$, context block $V$ now a stationary AR(1)
\emph{process} with observations $S_s=V_s+U_s$, $\mathrm{Var}\,U=
\tau^2$), single-letter two-dimensional records
$\hat{\mathbf Y}=AZ+N$ with per-consumer distortions $D_A,D_B$. The
eraser holds a context $\sigma$-algebra $\mathcal G$ from the
observation process (slice, causal prefix, full path --- GO-12's access
family). Coordinates: the joint conditional coordinate
$L_{AB}(\mathcal G)$ (weighted-program value of GO-11 Theorem~9 with
the conditional covariance induced by $\mathcal G$), the single-consumer
coordinates $L_A(\mathcal G),L_B(\mathcal G)$, and the taxes
\[
\mathrm{CT}_R \;=\; R_{AB}-\max(R_A,R_B),\qquad
\mathrm{CT}_W(\mathcal G)\;=\;L_{AB}(\mathcal G)-
\max\bigl(L_A(\mathcal G),L_B(\mathcal G)\bigr).
\]
$\mathrm{CT}_W$ is now a \emph{function of the eraser's access}: the
dynamic complementarity tax.

\section{Conjectures}

\begin{conjecture}[The matrix-$q$ reduction]\label{conj:matq}
For $m=2$ single-letter records, every context $\sigma$-algebra
$\mathcal G$ generated by a nonempty subset of the observations enters
the coordinates only through the conditional covariance
$\Sigma_{T\mid\mathcal G}$ --- the matrix analog of GO-12's
$q_{\mathcal G}$: the whitened pair $(\Sigma_T,\Sigma_{T\mid\mathcal
G})$ feeds Theorem~9's matrix water-level system unchanged. Candidate
proof is GO-12's channel-agnostic sufficiency argument verbatim
(project $T$ on the closed span of $\mathcal G$; the residual is
independent of the span; record noises never see $\mathcal G$). If it
holds, the access family comes for free: slice, causal prefix, and
full path become single-sample, Kalman fixed-lag, and noncausal-Wiener
\emph{conditional covariances} in the water-level system, with the
strict interpolation inherited from strict covariance ordering ---
provided the monotonicity of Theorem~9's value in
$\Sigma_{T\mid\mathcal G}$ (Loewner order) is established, which is a
genuine proof obligation, not a corollary.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[Staleness converts work-tax into rate-tax]
\label{conj:taxflow}
With a scalar context ($r=1$), all coordinates are functions of the
single number $q_{\mathcal G}$, hence so is
$\mathrm{CT}_W(q)$. Two anchors are nearly free: at perfect fresh
access $q\to q_{\min}$ the tax is GO-10's work tax, and as access
degrades completely, $q\to1$, every conditional coordinate tends to
its marginal counterpart, so $\mathrm{CT}_W(q)\to\mathrm{CT}_R$. The
conjecture is that the passage is \emph{continuous and monotone} in
$q$ on the active regime --- staleness continuously converts the work
tax into the rate tax, with the access-class family of GO-12 tracing
the same curve regardless of \emph{how} $q$ is produced (slice lag,
prefix lag, or noise level: access-class universality of the tax
curve). A refutation --- a non-monotone tax, or class-dependence
beyond $q$ --- would be a finding of equal rank.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{remark}
Conjecture~\ref{conj:taxflow}'s universality clause is a direct
corollary of Conjecture~\ref{conj:matq} when $r=1$ (everything is
$q$); it is stated separately because it survives even if the matrix
reduction needs extra hypotheses at $r\ge2$, and because it is the
operationally testable face: two access classes tuned to equal $q$
must show equal taxes --- an analytic-equality control of the
061/062/065 kind.
\end{remark}

\section{Questions}

\begin{question}
The spectral $m=2$ program: matrix water levels per frequency ---
GO-12 Conjecture~1 and GO-11 Theorem~9 jointly. Blocked on the same
library debt (Leiner--Gray 1974 / Wyner 1976--78 checks) plus a
multiterminal novelty flank: CEO/Heegard--Berger with degraded or
stale side information at two decoders, and the multi-decoder variants
of the Simeone--Permuter line, must be swept before any novelty
language.
\end{question}

\begin{question}
The binary twin: GO-11 Theorem~10's tilt-equation family under aged
context --- does the $(d_0,d_1)$ family absorb access classes through
a single effective $q$ the way the Gaussian theory does (the
$u=a*q$-convolution structure suggests yes, with $q$ entering the
convolution parameter)?
\end{question}

\begin{question}
The operational face: 058-lineage decode-threshold instruments with
two consumers and an aged context, gating (i) the tax at two access
classes tuned to equal $q$ (the universality control), and (ii) the
monotone tax curve across staleness levels. Instrument bias budget per
PROTOCOL \S5.1, with the paired-bias device of the 064 cross-net.
\end{question}
