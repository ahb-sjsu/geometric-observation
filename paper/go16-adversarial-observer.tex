\begin{abstract}
Every consumer in the program to date is benevolent: the encoder
compresses \emph{for} it. This campaign opens the complement: a
consumer of the encoder's record whose reading the encoder wants to
defeat, while the record simultaneously serves as the encoder's own
payoff instrument. The motivating instance is poker --- the opponent
reads your action record through a bounded model (a $k$-statistic
HUD), and range balancing, bluffing, and exploitation are all
statements about the geometry of that read --- but the object is
general: it is the \emph{negative-weight consumer}, and the
multi-consumer machinery of GO-10/11 meets it with a sign flip. We
define an LQG disclosure game, prove the two elementary structural
facts (the free-exploitation subspace is exactly the reader's kernel;
the budgeted reader's value is a Ky Fan $k$-sum, with rank
saturation), and report an opening numerical probe (deterministic,
disclosed, exploratory) whose four findings shape the conjecture
list: kernel-freeness confirmed exact; rank saturation confirmed to
six digits; the LQG game \emph{never randomizes} (deception is
shrinkage --- randomized bluffing is conjectured to be the shadow of
discrete action support); and the wholesale ``indifference = spectral
ties'' conjecture is \emph{refined at the probe}: ties appear only
where shielding is active at the reader's margin, exactly poker's
on-support-only indifference. No lower-bound face exists; no
registration is consumed; the novelty flank is owed before any seal.
\end{abstract}

\section{The object}

An observer in this program is a triple $O=(C,G,B)$ inducing a read
metric $P_C$ and a quotient. Everywhere so far the encoder's problem
is aligned: allocate fidelity to what the consumer reads. The
adversarial observer inverts the sign. The encoder's record $A$ ---
here, not a stored description but the encoder's \emph{observable
behavior}, accumulated over play --- is read by two parties:
\begin{itemize}
\item the encoder's own consumer (its payoff functional: actions must
  track the private state to earn value), and
\item an adversary $D$, who reads $A$ through a \emph{budgeted} read
  operator and converts what it resolves about the private state into
  the encoder's loss.
\end{itemize}
The encoder's objective is a two-consumer program with opposite
signs: minimize its own consumer's distortion while
\emph{maximizing} the adversary-consumer's distortion. Poker
vocabulary maps exactly: the ideal policy $S$ is maximal
exploitation; shielding is balance; the reader's kernel is the
bluffing space; reader indifference is the indifference condition
that defines mixed equilibria. The program-internal hook is
Conjecture~\ref{conj:signed}: this is the GO-10/11 $m{=}2$ region
continued to signed weight.

\section{The LQG disclosure game}

\begin{definition}[Disclosure game $\mathcal{D}(S,H,k,\lambda)$]
\label{def:game}
Private type $X\sim\mathcal N(0,I_n)$. The encoder commits to a
record policy
\[
A \;=\; F X + W, \qquad W\sim\mathcal N(0,\Sigma_w),\;
\Sigma_w\succeq 0 \text{ independent of } X,
\]
with $F\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$; $S\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$ is the
value-ideal policy and the encoder's \textbf{value loss} is
\[
L_{\mathrm{val}}(F,\Sigma_w) \;=\; \lVert F-S\rVert_F^2 + \tr\Sigma_w .
\]
The adversary chooses a rank-$k$ read $\Theta\in\mathbb R^{k\times m}$
(WLOG $\Theta\Theta^\top=I_k$), observes $Y=\Theta A$, and exploits
the statistic $Z=HX$ ($H\in\mathbb R^{p\times n}$); its gain --- the
encoder's \textbf{leakage} --- is the resolved variance
\[
R(\Theta;F,\Sigma_w) \;=\; \tr\operatorname{Cov}\!\big(\mathbb E[Z\mid Y]\big)
\;=\; \tr\!\Big( HF^\top\Theta^\top \big(\Theta N \Theta^\top\big)^{-1}
\Theta F H^\top \Big),
\qquad N = FF^\top+\Sigma_w .
\]
The encoder commits first (the population/HUD setting: the pool
adapts to the encoder's long-run record); the Stackelberg objective is
\[
J(F,\Sigma_w) \;=\; L_{\mathrm{val}}(F,\Sigma_w)
\;+\; \lambda \max_{\Theta} R(\Theta;F,\Sigma_w).
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}
The record is the payoff instrument: $L_{\mathrm{val}}$ charges
deviation of the \emph{transmitted signal itself} from the value
policy. This is the structural feature that separates the game from
privacy mechanisms (where the released signal has no intrinsic payoff
role) and from persuasion (where the sender's signal is costless).
The action-cost convention $\tr\Sigma_w$ (dither costs chips) is part
of the definition and is load-bearing for
Conjecture~\ref{conj:dither}.
\end{remark}

\section{Two elementary structural facts}

\begin{proposition}[Kernel-freeness: the free-exploitation subspace]
\label{prop:kernel}
Fix $\Theta$ and let $P=\Theta^\top\Theta$ be the projection onto the
read subspace of the record space $\mathbb R^m$. Every minimizer of
$J_\Theta = L_{\mathrm{val}} + \lambda R(\Theta;\cdot)$ satisfies
\[
(I-P)\,F^\star \;=\; (I-P)\,S,
\qquad
(I-P)\,\Sigma_w^\star\,(I-P) \;=\; 0
\;\;\big(\text{hence } \Sigma_w^\star = P\Sigma_w^\star P\big).
\]
The encoder implements its exploitative policy \emph{exactly} on
$\ker\Theta$ (dimension $m-k$) at zero leakage, and never dithers
there.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
$R$ depends on $F$ only through $\Theta F=\Theta PF$ and on
$\Sigma_w$ only through $\Theta\Sigma_w\Theta^\top$. The value loss
splits as $\lVert P(F-S)\rVert_F^2+\lVert(I-P)(F-S)\rVert_F^2$, so
setting $(I-P)F=(I-P)S$ removes the second term without touching $R$.
For the dither: $\tr\Sigma_w=\tr(P\Sigma_w P)+\tr((I-P)\Sigma_w(I-P))$,
and the second term has zero effect on $R$; optimality forces it to
zero, and a PSD matrix with a vanishing diagonal block has vanishing
off-diagonal blocks, giving $\Sigma_w=P\Sigma_w P$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[The budgeted reader's value is a Ky Fan sum]
\label{prop:kyfan}
With $G \;=\; N^{-1/2} F H^\top H F^\top N^{-1/2}$,
\[
\max_{\Theta\in\mathbb R^{k\times m}} R(\Theta;F,\Sigma_w)
\;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{k}\lambda_i(G) \;=\; \KF{G},
\]
attained by any $\Theta$ whose whitened row space
$N^{1/2}\Theta^\top$ spans the top-$k$ eigenspace of $G$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Substitute $U=N^{1/2}\Theta^\top$, so $\Theta N\Theta^\top=U^\top U$
and $\Theta F H^\top=U^\top M$ with $M=N^{-1/2}FH^\top$. Then
$R=\tr(M^\top P_U M)$ with $P_U$ the orthogonal projection onto
$\operatorname{col}(U)$, maximized over $k$-dimensional subspaces by
the top-$k$ eigenspace of $MM^\top=G$, with value the Ky Fan
$k$-sum.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Rank saturation: HUD width beyond the exploit rank
is wasted]
\label{cor:rank}
$\KF{G}$ is constant in $k$ for $k\ge\operatorname{rank}(HF^\top)$;
in particular a rank-one exploit statistic ($p=1$) makes every reader
budget $k\ge1$ equivalent.
\end{corollary}

The encoder's Stackelberg problem is therefore
\[
\min_{F,\;\Sigma_w\succeq0}\;
\lVert F-S\rVert_F^2+\tr\Sigma_w+\lambda\,\KF{G(F,\Sigma_w)},
\]
a Ky Fan minimization --- convex in $G$, not jointly convex in
$(F,\Sigma_w)$; its curvature structure is an open item below.

\section{The opening probe (exploratory, disclosed)}

\texttt{experiments/go16\_adversarial\_probe.py}, seed 20260821,
$n{=}m{=}6$, deterministic (numpy/scipy, L-BFGS-B with restarts);
result JSON committed at
\texttt{results/go16-adversarial-probe.json}. Diagonal $\Sigma_w$
(a subclass of Proposition~\ref{prop:kernel}'s general PSD dither).
No registration governs this run; every number is an exploratory
upper-bound-quality observation.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{(C1) Kernel-freeness, confirmed exact.} Fixed random
  $\Theta$ ($k{=}2$, $\lambda{=}1$): kernel residual
  $\lVert(I-P)(F^\star-S)\rVert = 2.2\times10^{-7}$, kernel dither
  $5\times10^{-16}$, against a read-subspace deviation of $0.545$.
\item \textbf{(C5) Rank saturation, confirmed.} $p{=}1$: $J$ is flat
  to six digits across $k=1..5$ ($5.024983$), with a $3.5\times10^{-5}$
  optimizer-noise uptick at $k{=}6$.
\item \textbf{(C4) The LQG game never dithers.} Across the full
  sweep ($\lambda\in\{0.01,0.3,1,3\}$, $k=1..6$): total dither
  $\le1.1\times10^{-4}$ against $O(1)$ value losses. Deception is
  implemented entirely by \emph{shrinkage} of $F$ toward reduced
  legibility, never by randomization.
\item \textbf{(C2) The indifference conjecture, refined.} The naive
  transplant of poker indifference --- ``at the optimum the
  $G$-spectrum ties at the $k$-th eigenvalue, making the reader
  indifferent at its margin'' --- is \emph{wrong as phrased}. Ties
  appear only where shielding is active at the reader's margin:
  relative gap $\lambda_k-\lambda_{k+1}$ over $\lambda_1$ is $0.48$
  at $(\lambda{=}1,k{=}1)$ (strict; the contested top direction is
  shielded but not down to $\lambda_2$) yet $2.6\times10^{-7}$ at
  $(\lambda{=}3,k{=}5)$ and $1.9\times10^{-3}$ at
  $(\lambda{=}0.3,k{=}5)$. This is exactly poker's structure:
  indifference holds \emph{on the support of the mixed strategy
  only}; off-support, strict inequalities persist.
\item \textbf{(C3) An activation threshold (unlooked-for).} At
  $\lambda{=}0.3$, $k\le3$: the encoder does not shield at all
  (value loss $\sim10^{-13}$) and simply pays the leak; shielding
  activates between $k{=}3$ and $k{=}4$, when the $\lambda$-weighted
  marginal spectral mass first exceeds the quadratic cost of moving
  $F$. There is a lazy regime --- ``the pool isn't reading enough to
  be worth balancing against'' --- with a computable boundary.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conjectures}

\begin{conjecture}[On-support indifference; complementary slackness]
\label{conj:ties}
At the Stackelberg optimum, generically:\;
$\lambda_k(G^\star)=\lambda_{k+1}(G^\star)$ if and only if the
shielding is active at the reader's marginal direction (the
subgradient of $\KF{\cdot}$ is non-singleton exactly on the contested
set), and the optimum partitions the record directions into
\emph{exploited} (unread: $\ker\Theta^\star$, carrying $S$ exactly),
\emph{contested} (read, shielded, tied spectrum --- the mixing
support), and \emph{conceded} (read, unshielded --- the lazy set).
The Ky Fan subdifferential machinery should make this a theorem in
the LQG game.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[Deception is shrinkage; bluffing is a support
artifact]
\label{conj:dither}
In $\mathcal{D}(S,H,k,\lambda)$ with the $\tr\Sigma_w$ action cost,
every optimum has $\Sigma_w^\star=0$: randomization is strictly
dominated by deterministic shrinkage. Randomized bluffing in real
games is then the shadow of \emph{discrete action support} (mixing is
the only way to interpolate between actions the alphabet forces
apart), not an intrinsic feature of optimal deception. The discrete
twin (Conjecture~\ref{conj:poker}) is where randomization must
reappear.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[The negative-weight consumer]
\label{conj:signed}
The encoder's objective is the GO-10/11 two-consumer program with a
signed weight: own-consumer distortion weighted $+1$, the
adversary-consumer's \emph{fidelity} weighted $+\lambda$ (i.e., its
distortion weighted $-\lambda$). The $m{=}2$ moment machinery of
GO-11/GO-13 (water levels, the convexity lemma of 074) continues to
signed weight with a characterizable loss of structure --- expected:
convexity survives in $G$ (Ky Fan) but the two-water-level geometry
does not survive the sign flip unmodified; what replaces it is the
three-way partition of Conjecture~\ref{conj:ties}.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[Commitment gap]
\label{conj:commit}
The Stackelberg value (encoder commits; the HUD adapts) and the
simultaneous saddle value differ in general; the gap is priced by the
contested set alone (kernel and conceded directions contribute
nothing). Poker reading: population play against an adapting pool is
the commitment game, and the value of ``playing your own game''
versus leveling wars lives only on the contested directions.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[The poker shadow --- the discrete twin]
\label{conj:poker}
In a solvable discrete game (Kuhn-scale, or a river toy game) with an
explicitly $k$-statistic-bounded opponent model: (i) the
free-exploitation subspace survives as the set of strategy deviations
invisible to the $k$ statistics; (ii) optimal play against the
bounded reader mixes \emph{only} where the reader's margin is
contested, and the mixing indifference conditions are the discrete
shadow of the spectral ties; (iii) per the GO-13 binary-twin
discipline, exact LQG identities should hold only approximately, with
measurable gaps to be reported, not hidden.
\end{conjecture}

\section{Novelty position (RECON-GRADE)}

One web pass, 2026-08-21; the full arXiv/DBLP flank with must-cite
list is owed before any seal. Conceded neighbors:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Quantitative information flow / $g$-leakage} (Alvim,
  Chatzikokolakis, McIver, Morgan, Palamidessi, Smith):
  adversary-objective-relative leakage via gain functions ---
  leakage-through-the-adversary's-functional is theirs. No budgeted
  subspace reader, no payoff-instrument record, no equilibrium
  geometry.
\item \textbf{Estimation-theoretic privacy} (privacy-utility
  tradeoffs against MMSE adversaries; Gaussian mechanisms; Fisher
  information privacy): additive-noise release against a quadratic
  adversary is well-plowed. The release there has no intrinsic
  payoff role, the adversary is unbudgeted (or budgeted in bits, not
  rank), and there is no Stackelberg spectral structure.
\item \textbf{Persuasion under rational inattention}
  (Bloedel--Segal): sender vs.\ attention-constrained receiver, with
  MI-costed attention and strategic attention manipulation --- the
  closest game-theoretic neighbor. Their receiver is a
  Shannon-channel chooser and the sender's signal is costless; the
  rank-$k$ geometric budget, the Ky Fan structure, kernel-freeness,
  and the tie/partition geometry do not appear.
\item \textbf{Poker theory itself}: exploitation priced by EV risk
  (restricted Nash, safe exploitation) --- never by read geometry.
\end{itemize}
The claimed open conjunction: \emph{rank-budgeted subspace reader
(Ky Fan values) $\times$ record-as-payoff-instrument $\times$
commitment saddle geometry (kernel-freeness / three-way partition /
on-support ties) $\times$ the discrete game instantiation}.
