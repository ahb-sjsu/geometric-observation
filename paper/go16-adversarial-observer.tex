\begin{abstract}
The adversarial observer is the program's first anti-aligned consumer:
a rank-$k$-budgeted reader of the encoder's own record, where the
record is simultaneously the encoder's payoff instrument. v0.1 posed
the LQG disclosure game and conjectured a partition/tie structure at
its optimum. This version proves it, through a reduction the
conjecture did not anticipate: leakage depends on the policy
$(F,\Sigma_w)$ only through the \textbf{revelation operator}
$K = F^\top N^{-1}F$, and the minimal value cost of achieving $K$ is
\emph{exactly linear}, $\tr(S(I-K)S^\top)$, attained by
\textbf{shrink-and-dither} ($F = SK$, $\Sigma_w = SK(I-K)S^\top$).
The game is therefore a convex semidefinite program with a bilinear
saddle against the reader's mixed attention operator
(Theorem~\ref{thm:reduction}). The saddle conditions yield the
partition: directions are \emph{revealed}, \emph{blacked out}, or
\emph{contested}, contested directions are priced to encoder
indifference, and fractional attention forces the spectral tie
$\lambda_k = \lambda_{k+1}$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:partition}). At
diagonal scope the solution is closed-form: a water level $t^\ast$,
contested SNRs $\rho_i = t^\ast/\mu_i$, reader mixing frequencies
$\theta_i = s_i^2/(\lambda\mu_i)$, and \textbf{tie iff contested
$\neq\emptyset$} (Theorem~\ref{thm:diagonal}) --- each side's mixture
pinned by the other's indifference, exactly the mixed-equilibrium
structure of poker. Two v0.1 items fall: Conjecture~2 (never-dither)
is \textbf{refuted} --- fractional revelation \emph{requires} dither,
and the v0.1 probe's contrary finding is explained by its instances
(their optima are near-projections needing $\approx$no dither; the
v0.2 jitter diagnosis was itself wrong and is corrected on the
R-IND-5 record) --- and Conjecture~4 (commitment gap) collapses
\emph{for the whitened-attention game}: with the reader committing to
a mixed attention operator, minimax $=$ maximin; the fixed-instrument
$\Theta$-mixture game is left open. The discrete twin (binary
alphabet) is settled at probe level: the tie survives exactly, the
pricing generalizes, mixing is the carrier of dither, and a fifth
class appears that LQG forbids. Verification: numerical harness ALL
PASS 9/9 + a diagnosis measurement
(\texttt{experiments/go16\_verify\_partition.py}); twin probe ALL
PASS 4/4 (\texttt{experiments/go16\_discrete\_twin.py}); six lemmas
machine-checked in Lean~4/Mathlib
(\texttt{lean/ObservationTheory/AdversarialObserver.lean}, built
clean on Atlas, zero \texttt{sorry}); fresh-context R-IND-5 pass
complete --- the mathematical core survived intact, and the seven
findings (2 exposition errors, 3 gaps, 2 overclaims) are addressed in
this version. Novelty flank on record: the composite game is
unoccupied; the SDP-ization machinery is imported
(Sayin--Akyol--Ba\c{s}ar; Tamura), the tie's optimization ancestor is
Overton--Womersley coalescence, and the partition/pricing/water-level
theorem is the headline novelty.
\end{abstract}

\section{The object}

An observer in this program is a triple $O=(C,G,B)$ inducing a read
metric $P_C$. Everywhere so far the encoder compresses \emph{for}
its consumer. Here the encoder's record $A$ --- its observable
behavior accumulated over play --- is read by two parties: the
encoder's own payoff functional (actions must track the private
state to earn value), and an adversary $D$ who reads $A$ through a
\emph{budgeted} read operator and converts what it resolves into the
encoder's loss. The encoder's objective is a two-consumer program
with opposite signs. Poker vocabulary maps exactly: the ideal policy
$S$ is maximal exploitation; shielding is balance; the reader's
kernel is the bluffing space; reader indifference is the
indifference condition that defines mixed equilibria.

\section{The LQG disclosure game}

\begin{definition}[Disclosure game $\mathcal{D}(S,H,k,\lambda)$]
\label{def:game}
Private type $X\sim\mathcal N(0,I_n)$. The encoder commits to
\[
A = F X + W, \qquad W\sim\mathcal N(0,\Sigma_w),\;
\Sigma_w\succeq 0 \perp X,
\]
with $F\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$, value-ideal $S\in\mathbb
R^{m\times n}$, and \textbf{value loss}
$L_{\mathrm{val}} = \lVert F-S\rVert_F^2 + \tr\Sigma_w$. The
adversary picks a rank-$k$ read $\Theta$ (WLOG
$\Theta\Theta^\top=I_k$), observes $Y=\Theta A$, and exploits
$Z=HX$; the encoder's \textbf{leakage} is the resolved variance
$R(\Theta) = \tr\operatorname{Cov}(\mathbb E[Z\mid Y])$. With
$N = FF^\top+\Sigma_w$ and the encoder committing first (the
population/HUD setting),
\[
J(F,\Sigma_w) \;=\; L_{\mathrm{val}}
\;+\; \lambda \max_{\Theta} R(\Theta;F,\Sigma_w).
\]
Throughout, $M := H^\top H$ (the exploit Gram) and
$\Sigma_S := S^\top S$ (the value Gram). Regularity: policies with
$N\succ0$; singular $N$ is handled by the $\varepsilon I$ limit,
which changes no infimum.
\end{definition}

\section{Elementary structure (v0.1, kept)}

\begin{proposition}[Kernel-freeness]\label{prop:kernel}
For fixed $\Theta$ with read projection $P=\Theta^\top\Theta$, every
minimizer satisfies $(I-P)F^\star = (I-P)S$ and
$\Sigma_w^\star = P\Sigma_w^\star P$: the free-exploitation subspace
is exactly $\ker\Theta$ (dimension $m-k$), carrying the exploitative
policy in full, with no dither.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[The budgeted reader's value]\label{prop:kyfan}
$\max_\Theta R = \sum_{i=1}^k \lambda_i(G) = \KF{G}$ with
$G = N^{-1/2}FH^\top H F^\top N^{-1/2}$, attained on the top-$k$
eigenspace of $G$; $\KF{G}$ is constant in $k$ beyond
$\operatorname{rank}(HF^\top)$ (rank saturation).
\end{proposition}

Proofs as in v0.1 (the substitution $U = N^{1/2}\Theta^\top$ and Ky
Fan's maximum principle).

\section{The revelation reduction}

\begin{definition}[Revelation operator]
$K = K(F,\Sigma_w) := F^\top N^{-1} F \in \mathbb R^{n\times n}$,
the posterior precision gain about $X$ from the full record:
$\operatorname{Cov}(X\mid A) = I - K$. Always $0\preceq K\preceq I$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Revelation reduction]\label{thm:reduction}
\emph{(i) (Sufficiency of $K$.)} The leakage spectrum is a function
of $K$ alone: the nonzero eigenvalues of $G$ and of
$M^{1/2}KM^{1/2}$ coincide, so $\KF{G} = \KF{M^{1/2}KM^{1/2}}$.

\emph{(ii) (Linear cost.)} For $m\ge n$ and $S$ of full column rank,
the minimal value cost to achieve revelation $K$ is
\[
C(K) \;=\; \tr\!\big(S(I-K)S^\top\big),
\]
attained by the \textbf{shrink-and-dither policy}
$F^\star = SK$, $\Sigma_w^\star = SK(I-K)S^\top$ (so
$N^\star = SKS^\top$). ($K$ with nontrivial kernel: by the
$\varepsilon$-limit; $m<n$: the constraint
$\operatorname{rank} K\le m$ appears and is left open.)

\emph{(iii) (Convexity and the saddle.)} The game reduces to the
convex semidefinite program
\[
\min_{0\preceq K\preceq I}\;
\tr\big(\Sigma_S(I-K)\big) + \lambda\,\KF{M^{1/2}KM^{1/2}}
\;=\;
\min_{0\preceq K\preceq I}\;\max_{W\in\mathcal W_k}\;
\mathcal L(K,W),
\]
$\mathcal L(K,W) := \tr(\Sigma_S(I-K)) + \lambda\tr(W
M^{1/2}KM^{1/2})$, $\mathcal W_k := \{0\preceq W\preceq I,\ \tr W =
k\}$; both feasible sets are compact convex and $\mathcal L$ is
bilinear, so a saddle point $(K^\ast, W^\ast)$ exists and minimax
equals maximin.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\emph{(i)} With $B = N^{-1/2}F$ we have $G = BMB^\top$ and
$K = B^\top B$; the nonzero spectra of $BMB^\top$ and
$M^{1/2}B^\top B M^{1/2}$ coincide ($XY$ vs.\ $YX$). Top-$k$ sums of
equal nonzero spectra of PSD matrices agree.

\emph{(ii) Lower bound.} Write $F = N^{1/2}B$, $P = N^{1/2}\succ0$,
$Z = SB^\top$, $Q = \sym Z = \tfrac12(Z+Z^\top)$. Then
\[
\text{cost}
= \lVert N^{1/2}B - S\rVert_F^2 + \tr N - \tr(FF^\top)
= \tr(SS^\top) + \tr P^2 - 2\tr(PQ),
\]
using $\tr(B^\top N B) = \tr(N BB^\top)$ (which cancels) and
$\tr(PZ) = \tr(PQ)$ (symmetric $\times$ antisymmetric is
traceless). Since $\tr((P-Q)^2)\ge0$ for symmetric $P-Q$,
$\tr P^2 - 2\tr(PQ) \ge -\tr Q^2$. Finally
\[
\tr Q^2 = \tfrac12\big[\tr(Z^2) + \tr(ZZ^\top)\big]
\;\le\; \tr(ZZ^\top) = \tr(SB^\top B S^\top) = \tr(SKS^\top),
\]
because $\tr(ZZ^\top)-\tr(Z^2) = \tfrac12\lVert
Z-Z^\top\rVert_F^2\ge0$. The converse thus uses \emph{two}
inequalities, both machine-checked: $\tr(XX^\top)\ge0$ (Lean:
\texttt{trace\_mul\_transpose\_self\_nonneg}, applied to symmetric
$X = P-Q$ and to $Z - Z^\top$) and $\tr(Z^2)\le\tr(ZZ^\top)$ (Lean:
\texttt{trace\_sq\_le\_trace\_mul\_transpose}). Hence cost
$\ge \tr(SS^\top) - \tr(SKS^\top) = \tr(S(I-K)S^\top)$.

\emph{Achievability.} For $F^\star = SK$, $\Sigma_w^\star =
SK(I-K)S^\top$ (PSD since $\operatorname{spec}K\subset[0,1]$):
$N^\star = SK^2S^\top + SK(I-K)S^\top = SKS^\top$ by the ring
identity $K^2 + K(I-K) = K$ \emph{(Lean: \texttt{revelation\_key},
\texttt{revelation\_variance} --- corrected from v0.2's erroneous
citation of the telescope identity, R-IND-5 finding 11)}; the cost
telescopes to $\tr(S(I-K)S^\top)$ by $(I-K)^2 + K(I-K) = I-K$
\emph{(Lean: \texttt{shrink\_dither\_key},
\texttt{shrink\_dither\_cost})}. $K$-verification (display corrected
from v0.2, R-IND-5 finding 4): with $S = U\Sigma V^\top$ (thin SVD;
$V$ is $n\times n$ orthogonal by full column rank, $U$ has
orthonormal columns) and $K\succ0$,
\[
N^\star = U(\Sigma V^\top K V \Sigma)U^\top,\qquad
(N^\star)^{+} = U(\Sigma V^\top K V \Sigma)^{-1}U^\top,
\]
(the four Moore--Penrose conditions hold since $U$ has orthonormal
columns), whence
\[
F^{\star\top}(N^\star)^{+}F^\star
= KV\Sigma(\Sigma V^\top K V \Sigma)^{-1}\Sigma V^\top K
= KV(V^\top K V)^{-1}V^\top K = K,
\]
using $\Sigma(\Sigma B\Sigma)^{-1}\Sigma = B^{-1}$ with
$B = V^\top KV$ and $VV^\top = I$. Singular $K$: by the
$\varepsilon$-limit (asserted-standard prose; the harness verifies
exact attainment for singular and projection $K$ directly, so the
identity in fact holds beyond the stated scope --- R-IND-5
finding~4).

\emph{(iii)} $\KF{\cdot}$ is the support function of $\mathcal W_k$
(Ky Fan), giving the inner max; linear-plus-support-function is
convex; Sion/von Neumann gives the saddle.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Deception requires dither; v0.1 Conjecture 2
\textbf{refuted}]\label{cor:dither}
A noiseless record ($\Sigma_w=0$, $F$ full row rank) has $K =
F^\top(FF^\top)^{-1}F$ \emph{idempotent}: revelation is a
projection, all-or-nothing per direction --- rescaling a noiseless
record shields nothing (the reader renormalizes). Fractional
revelation --- which the optimum generically demands
(Theorem~\ref{thm:diagonal}) --- is achievable \emph{only} with
$\Sigma_w\neq0$. Optimal deception is shrink-\emph{and}-dither:
randomization is intrinsic, not a discrete-support artifact.
\textbf{v0.1's Conjecture 2 asserted the opposite; it is refuted by
this corollary} --- the diagonal instances demand dither trace $0.19$
at the optimum. \textbf{The diagnosis of the v0.1 probe finding (C4,
``never dithers''), however, has itself been corrected twice, and
both corrections stay on the record.} v0.2 attributed C4 to an
instrument artifact ($\varepsilon I$ jitter supplying free noise).
The R-IND-5 pass refuted that mechanism quantitatively (the jitter
route books blackout-level cost at mid-range revelation --- finding
8), and the V10 measurement settles it: at the v0.1 probe's own
$(\lambda,k)$ cells, the exact optima are \emph{near-projections}
(fractional mass $\le 10^{-13}$, required dither $\le 2\times
10^{-12}$ at three of four cells; $0.11$ and $0.027$ at the fourth).
The v0.1 instances genuinely needed $\approx$no dither; they were
\emph{unrepresentative}, not mismeasured. The same measurement
yields an unlooked-for structural observation: the contested phase
appears to concentrate on \emph{commuting-aligned} $(\Sigma_S, M)$
pairs (the diagonal family and its rotations), with generic
non-commuting instances optimizing at near-projections --- folded
into Conjecture~\ref{conj:converse}. The v0.1 poker-facing reading
still inverts as v0.2 said: mixing in discrete games is the shadow
of the dither requirement, now confirmed by the twin probe
(\S\ref{sec:twin}).
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}[No commitment gap at LQG scope; v0.1 Conjecture 4
resolved]\label{cor:commit}
By Theorem~\ref{thm:reduction}(iii), minimax $=$ maximin \emph{for
the reduced game in which the reader's strategy is the whitened
attention operator $W$} --- equivalently, a reader who may condition
its read on the record's law $N$ (the population/HUD setting: the
pool reads the frequencies of the record actually played). In that
game, encoder commitment is worthless. \textbf{Scope correction
(R-IND-5 finding 9):} this does \emph{not} establish the claim for
the concrete game in which the reader commits to a mixture over
fixed instruments $\Theta$ \emph{without} knowledge of $N$: the map
from $\Theta$ to attention, $P_U = N^{1/2}\Theta^\top(\Theta
N\Theta^\top)^{-1}\Theta N^{1/2}$, depends on the encoder's $N$, so
a committed $\Theta$-mixture cannot enforce a fixed $W^\ast$ across
encoder deviations. Whether a commitment gap exists in the
fixed-instrument game is \textbf{open}. What stands: the reader must
mix (projections --- the extreme points of $\mathcal W_k$ ---
announced first are exploitable), and the poker structure is
recovered, not assumed, in the $N$-conditioned reading.
\end{corollary}

\section{The partition/tie theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Partition and ties, general scope]
\label{thm:partition}
Let $(K^\ast,W^\ast)$ be any saddle point of $\mathcal L$, and set
$G^\ast = M^{1/2}K^\ast M^{1/2}$,
$\Delta^\ast = \lambda M^{1/2}W^\ast M^{1/2} - \Sigma_S$. Then:
\begin{itemize}
\item[(i)] \emph{(Pricing.)} $K^\ast$ minimizes the \emph{linear}
functional $\tr(K\Delta^\ast)$ over $0\preceq K\preceq I$: on the
negative eigenspace of $\Delta^\ast$, $K^\ast = I$ (\textbf{revealed}
--- the conceded directions, where even full attention is priced
below value, together with the submerged directions the reader
ignores); on the positive eigenspace, $K^\ast = 0$
(\textbf{blacked out}); every eigenvector of $K^\ast$ with
fractional eigenvalue lies in $\ker\Delta^\ast$
(\textbf{contested}): on contested directions the reader's mixed
attention prices revelation to exact encoder indifference,
$\lambda\, M^{1/2}W^\ast M^{1/2} = \Sigma_S$.
\item[(ii)] \emph{(Attention.)} $W^\ast$ maximizes $\tr(WG^\ast)$
over $\mathcal W_k$: $W^\ast = P_{>} + Z$ with $P_{>}$ the projector
onto eigenvalues $>\lambda_k(G^\ast)$ and $Z$ supported on the
$\lambda_k(G^\ast)$-eigenspace.
\item[(iii)] \emph{(Tie.)} If $W^\ast$ has an eigenvalue in $(0,1)$
--- the reader genuinely mixes --- then
$\lambda_k(G^\ast) = \lambda_{k+1}(G^\ast)$.
\end{itemize}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
(i) and (ii) are the saddle conditions; both subproblems are linear
over spectral polytopes, so optimizers are as described (for (ii),
Ky Fan's maximum principle with its equality cases). (iii): suppose
$\lambda_k > \lambda_{k+1}$. The eigenvalue group of value
$\lambda_k$ then ends at position $k$; maximizers put weight $1$ on
all eigendirections with value $>\lambda_k$ (count $k-r$, where $r$
is the multiplicity of $\lambda_k$) and the remaining budget $r$
exactly fills the group --- forcing $W^\ast = P_{\ge}$, a
projection. Contrapositive gives the claim.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Diagonal closed form: the attention water level]
\label{thm:diagonal}
Let $\Sigma_S = \operatorname{diag}(s_1^2,\dots,s_n^2)$,
$M = \operatorname{diag}(\mu_1,\dots,\mu_n)$, under genericity: (G1)
$\mu_i$ distinct, positive; (G2) $\lambda\mu_i \neq s_i^2$ for all
$i$. Then the optimum of the reduced program is diagonal,
$K^\ast = \operatorname{diag}(\rho_i)$, with a \textbf{water level}
$t^\ast\ge0$ at a breakpoint $t^\ast = \mu_j$, and the solution falls
in exactly one of two regimes: the \textbf{fractional regime} ---
condition (G3): the attention-budget equation resolves with strictly
interior slack --- in which the contested set is nonempty, or the
\textbf{integral regime}, in which every $\theta_i\in\{0,1\}$ and the
contested set is empty. (Scoping corrected from v0.2, whose (G3)
hypothesis made the empty-contested branch vacuous --- R-IND-5
finding 7: G3 \emph{forces} contested $\neq\emptyset$; the two
regimes are now stated as the exhaustive alternative.) With
$g_i := \mu_i\rho_i$:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Conceded} ($\lambda\mu_i < s_i^2$, $\mu_i > t^\ast$):
$\rho_i = 1$, $g_i = \mu_i$, attention $\theta_i = 1$. (The lazy
regime: shielding costs more than the priced leak.)
\item \textbf{Contested} ($\lambda\mu_i > s_i^2$, $\mu_i > t^\ast$):
$\rho_i = t^\ast/\mu_i\in(0,1)$, $g_i = t^\ast$ exactly, and
$\theta_i = s_i^2/(\lambda\mu_i)\in(0,1)$: \emph{the reader's mixing
frequency equals the ratio of shielding cost to priced attention ---
pinned by encoder indifference.}
\item \textbf{Level} ($\mu_j = t^\ast$): $\rho_j = 1$, absorbing the
attention slack $\theta_j = k - \#\{\text{conceded}\} -
\sum_{\text{contested}} s_i^2/(\lambda\mu_i)$, capped by
$\min(1, s_j^2/(\lambda\mu_j))$; $\theta_j\in(0,1)$ in the
fractional regime, $\theta_j\in\{0,1\}$ in the integral one.
\item \textbf{Submerged} ($\mu_i < t^\ast$): $\rho_i = 1$, $g_i =
\mu_i$, $\theta_i = 0$: fully revealed, unread --- free exploitation
by budget exhaustion rather than by structural invisibility.
\end{itemize}
Moreover the \textbf{tie} $\lambda_k(G^\ast) = \lambda_{k+1}(G^\ast)
= t^\ast$ holds \emph{iff} the contested set is nonempty ---
equivalently, iff the solution is in the fractional regime; in the
integral regime (e.g.\ $\mu = (4, 2.5, 1.8)$,
$s^2 = (5,5,5)$, $\lambda = 1$, $k = 2$: all conceded,
$\theta = (1,1,0)$, spectrum strict --- the R-IND-5 verifier's
witness) the top-$k$ spectrum is strict under (G1).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\emph{Diagonality (symmetrization).} The reduced program is convex
(Theorem~\ref{thm:reduction}(iii)) and invariant under $K\mapsto
DKD$ for every sign matrix $D = \operatorname{diag}(\pm1)$ (both
$\Sigma_S$ and $M$ commute with $D$; the spectrum of
$M^{1/2}DKDM^{1/2} = D M^{1/2}KM^{1/2}D$ is unchanged). Averaging
$K$ over the $2^n$ sign conjugations preserves feasibility, does not
increase the convex objective, and returns
$\operatorname{diag}(K)$; a diagonal optimum exists.

\emph{Per-coordinate cost.} At diagonal $K$, coordinate $i$ carries
SNR $\rho_i$ at cost $s_i^2(1-\rho_i)$ --- the scalar case of
Theorem~\ref{thm:reduction}(ii), by the exact completion of squares
$(f-s)^2 + f^2(1-\rho)/\rho = s^2(1-\rho) + (f-\rho s)^2/\rho$
\emph{(Lean: \texttt{scalar\_shielding\_identity},
\texttt{scalar\_shielding\_cost\_bound})}, minimized at the
shrink-and-dither point $f=\rho s$, $w = \rho(1-\rho)s^2$.

\emph{Water level.} With $\phi_k(g) = \min_t\,[kt +
\sum_i(g_i-t)_+]$, the program is jointly convex in $(\rho,t)$ and
separable at fixed $t$: coordinate $i$'s objective
$s_i^2(1-\rho_i) + \lambda(\mu_i\rho_i - t)_+$ has constant slopes
$-s_i^2 + \lambda\mu_i > 0$ above the kink and $-s_i^2 < 0$ below
(G2), so its minimum is \emph{at} the kink $\rho_i = \min(1,
t/\mu_i)$ when $\lambda\mu_i > s_i^2$, and at $\rho_i = 1$
otherwise: linear-versus-linear forces bang-bang-to-the-kink ---
this is where the tie mechanism lives. The value $J(t)$ is
piecewise linear with breakpoints $\{\mu_i\}$, so its minimum sits
at a breakpoint $t^\ast = \mu_j$ (generically unique); the
subgradient condition $0\in\partial J(t^\ast)$ \emph{is} the
attention budget $\sum_i\theta_i = k$ with the stated $\theta$, and
the caps are exactly the encoder's first-order conditions at
$\rho_i = 1$ boundaries.

\emph{Tie.} If contested $=\{i_1,\dots,i_c\}$, $c\ge1$: the
spectrum of $G^\ast$ has the conceded values above $t^\ast$ (count
$a$), then the tied group $\{t^\ast\}$ of multiplicity $c+1$
(contested plus level), then the submerged values. The budget gives
$k = a + \sum_{\text{contested}}\theta_i + \theta_j$ with every
$\theta$ in the group fractional, so $a < k < a + c + 1$, hence both
$\lambda_k$ and $\lambda_{k+1}$ lie in the tied group:
$\lambda_k = \lambda_{k+1} = t^\ast$. If contested $=\emptyset$ the
spectrum is $\{\mu_i\}$, distinct by (G1): strict.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The poker reading]
Contested directions are the mixing support: there, and only there,
(a) the reader mixes (fractional $\theta$ = randomized attention ---
bluff-catching frequencies), (b) the encoder part-shields
(fractional $\rho$ = balanced ranges), (c) each side's frequency is
pinned by the \emph{other's} indifference
($\theta_i = s_i^2/(\lambda\mu_i)$; $g_i = t^\ast$), and (d) the
spectrum ties --- indifference holds on the support only, exactly as
in poker. Conceded directions are ``too cheap to balance''
(the lazy regime the probe found); submerged directions are
exploitation for free by budget exhaustion; $\ker\Theta$ (fixed
reads) was v0.1's structural free-exploitation and survives as the
$W$-null space.
\end{remark}

\item \textbf{A fifth class appears that LQG forbids}:

\end{itemize}
