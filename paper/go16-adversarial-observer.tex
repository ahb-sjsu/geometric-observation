\begin{abstract}
The adversarial observer is the program's first anti-aligned consumer:
a rank-$k$-budgeted reader of the encoder's own record, where the
record is simultaneously the encoder's payoff instrument. v0.1 posed
the LQG disclosure game and conjectured a partition/tie structure at
its optimum. This version proves it, through a reduction the
conjecture did not anticipate: leakage depends on the policy
$(F,\Sigma_w)$ only through the \textbf{revelation operator}
$K = F^\top N^{-1}F$, and the minimal value cost of achieving $K$ is
\emph{exactly linear}, $\tr(S(I-K)S^\top)$, attained by
\textbf{shrink-and-dither} ($F = SK$, $\Sigma_w = SK(I-K)S^\top$).
The game is therefore a convex semidefinite program with a bilinear
saddle against the reader's mixed attention operator
(Theorem~\ref{thm:reduction}). The saddle conditions yield the
partition: directions are \emph{revealed}, \emph{blacked out}, or
\emph{contested}, contested directions are priced to encoder
indifference, and fractional attention forces the spectral tie
$\lambda_k = \lambda_{k+1}$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:partition}). At
diagonal scope the solution is closed-form: a water level $t^\ast$,
contested SNRs $\rho_i = t^\ast/\mu_i$, reader mixing frequencies
$\theta_i = s_i^2/(\lambda\mu_i)$, a blackout regime at $t^\ast=0$,
and \textbf{tie iff some coordinate is contested or blacked out}
(Theorem~\ref{thm:diagonal}; corrected 2026-11-20 --- the v0.2--v0.5
statement ``tie iff contested $\neq\emptyset$'', sealed in GO-P-2026-090,
is false on an open set, NEG-17) --- on contested coordinates, given
the level $t^\ast$, each side's mixture is pinned by the other's
indifference, the mixed-equilibrium structure of poker (in blackout the
reader's attention is bounded below, and pinned only when $J'(0+)=0$). Two v0.1 items fall: Conjecture~2 (never-dither)
is \textbf{refuted} --- fractional revelation \emph{requires} dither,
and the v0.1 probe's contrary finding is explained by its instances
(their optima are near-projections needing $\approx$no dither; the
v0.2 jitter diagnosis was itself wrong and is corrected on the
R-IND-5 record) --- and Conjecture~4 (commitment gap) collapses
\emph{for the whitened-attention game}: with the reader committing to
a mixed attention operator, minimax $=$ maximin; the fixed-instrument
$\Theta$-mixture game is settled at commuting scope for $k=1$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:nogap}) and open otherwise. The discrete twin (binary
alphabet) is settled at probe level: the tie survives exactly, the
pricing generalizes, mixing is the carrier of dither, and a fifth
class appears that LQG forbids. Verification: the governed harness
(\texttt{experiments/go16\_verify\_partition.py}, ALL PASS 9/9) passed
the statement since refuted, its V6 gate being circular (NEG-17); the
corrected Theorem~\ref{thm:diagonal} is netted by
\texttt{experiments/verify\_go16\_diagonal.py} (9/9, in CI); twin probe ALL
PASS 4/4 (\texttt{experiments/go16\_discrete\_twin.py}); eight theorems
machine-checked in Lean~4/Mathlib
(\texttt{lean/ObservationTheory/AdversarialObserver.lean}, built
clean on Atlas, zero \texttt{sorry}); a fresh-context R-IND-5 pass on

\end{abstract}

\section{The object}

An observer in this program is a triple $O=(C,G,B)$ inducing a read
metric $P_C$. Everywhere so far the encoder compresses \emph{for}
its consumer. Here the encoder's record $A$ --- its observable
behavior accumulated over play --- is read by two parties: the
encoder's own payoff functional (actions must track the private
state to earn value), and an adversary $D$ who reads $A$ through a
\emph{budgeted} read operator and converts what it resolves into the
encoder's loss. The encoder's objective is a two-consumer program
with opposite signs. Poker vocabulary maps exactly: the ideal policy
$S$ is maximal exploitation; shielding is balance; the reader's
kernel is the bluffing space; reader indifference is the
indifference condition that defines mixed equilibria.

\section{The LQG disclosure game}

\begin{definition}[Disclosure game $\mathcal{D}(S,H,k,\lambda)$]
\label{def:game}
Private type $X\sim\mathcal N(0,I_n)$. The encoder commits to
\[
A = F X + W, \qquad W\sim\mathcal N(0,\Sigma_w),\;
\Sigma_w\succeq 0 \perp X,
\]
with $F\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$, value-ideal $S\in\mathbb
R^{m\times n}$, and \textbf{value loss}
$L_{\mathrm{val}} = \lVert F-S\rVert_F^2 + \tr\Sigma_w$. The
adversary picks a rank-$k$ read $\Theta$ (WLOG
$\Theta\Theta^\top=I_k$), observes $Y=\Theta A$, and exploits
$Z=HX$; the encoder's \textbf{leakage} is the resolved variance
$R(\Theta) = \tr\operatorname{Cov}(\mathbb E[Z\mid Y])$. With
$N = FF^\top+\Sigma_w$ and the encoder committing first (the
population/HUD setting),
\[
J(F,\Sigma_w) \;=\; L_{\mathrm{val}}
\;+\; \lambda \max_{\Theta} R(\Theta;F,\Sigma_w).
\]
Throughout, $M := H^\top H$ (the exploit Gram) and
$\Sigma_S := S^\top S$ (the value Gram). Regularity: policies with
$N\succ0$; singular $N$ is handled by the $\varepsilon I$ limit,
which changes no infimum.
\end{definition}

\section{Elementary structure (v0.1, kept)}

\begin{proposition}[Kernel-freeness]\label{prop:kernel}
For fixed $\Theta$ with read projection $P=\Theta^\top\Theta$, every
minimizer satisfies $(I-P)F^\star = (I-P)S$ and
$\Sigma_w^\star = P\Sigma_w^\star P$: the free-exploitation subspace
is exactly $\ker\Theta$ (dimension $m-k$), carrying the exploitative
policy in full, with no dither.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[The budgeted reader's value]\label{prop:kyfan}
$\max_\Theta R = \sum_{i=1}^k \lambda_i(G) = \KF{G}$ with
$G = N^{-1/2}FH^\top H F^\top N^{-1/2}$, attained on the top-$k$
eigenspace of $G$; $\KF{G}$ is constant in $k$ beyond
$\operatorname{rank}(HF^\top)$ (rank saturation).
\end{proposition}

Proofs as in v0.1 (the substitution $U = N^{1/2}\Theta^\top$ and Ky
Fan's maximum principle).

\section{The revelation reduction}

\begin{definition}[Revelation operator]
$K = K(F,\Sigma_w) := F^\top N^{-1} F \in \mathbb R^{n\times n}$,
the posterior precision gain about $X$ from the full record:
$\operatorname{Cov}(X\mid A) = I - K$. Always $0\preceq K\preceq I$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Revelation reduction]\label{thm:reduction}
\emph{(i) (Sufficiency of $K$.)} The leakage spectrum is a function
of $K$ alone: the nonzero eigenvalues of $G$ and of
$M^{1/2}KM^{1/2}$ coincide, so $\KF{G} = \KF{M^{1/2}KM^{1/2}}$.

\emph{(ii) (Linear cost.)} For $m\ge n$ and $S$ of full column rank,
the minimal value cost to achieve revelation $K$ is
\[
C(K) \;=\; \tr\!\big(S(I-K)S^\top\big),
\]
attained by the \textbf{shrink-and-dither policy}
$F^\star = SK$, $\Sigma_w^\star = SK(I-K)S^\top$ (so
$N^\star = SKS^\top$). ($K$ with nontrivial kernel: by the
$\varepsilon$-limit; $m<n$: the constraint
$\operatorname{rank} K\le m$ appears and is left open.)

\emph{(iii) (Convexity and the saddle.)} The game reduces to the
convex semidefinite program
\[
\min_{0\preceq K\preceq I}\;
\tr\big(\Sigma_S(I-K)\big) + \lambda\,\KF{M^{1/2}KM^{1/2}}
\;=\;
\min_{0\preceq K\preceq I}\;\max_{W\in\mathcal W_k}\;
\mathcal L(K,W),
\]
$\mathcal L(K,W) := \tr(\Sigma_S(I-K)) + \lambda\tr(W
M^{1/2}KM^{1/2})$, $\mathcal W_k := \{0\preceq W\preceq I,\ \tr W =
k\}$; both feasible sets are compact convex and $\mathcal L$ is
bilinear, so a saddle point $(K^\ast, W^\ast)$ exists and minimax
equals maximin.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\emph{(i)} With $B = N^{-1/2}F$ we have $G = BMB^\top$ and
$K = B^\top B$; the nonzero spectra of $BMB^\top$ and
$M^{1/2}B^\top B M^{1/2}$ coincide ($XY$ vs.\ $YX$). Top-$k$ sums of
equal nonzero spectra of PSD matrices agree.

\emph{(ii) Lower bound.} Write $F = N^{1/2}B$, $P = N^{1/2}\succ0$,
$Z = SB^\top$, $Q = \sym Z = \tfrac12(Z+Z^\top)$. Then
\[
\text{cost}
= \lVert N^{1/2}B - S\rVert_F^2 + \tr N - \tr(FF^\top)
= \tr(SS^\top) + \tr P^2 - 2\tr(PQ),
\]
using $\tr(B^\top N B) = \tr(N BB^\top)$ (which cancels) and
$\tr(PZ) = \tr(PQ)$ (symmetric $\times$ antisymmetric is
traceless). Since $\tr((P-Q)^2)\ge0$ for symmetric $P-Q$,
$\tr P^2 - 2\tr(PQ) \ge -\tr Q^2$. Finally
\[
\tr Q^2 = \tfrac12\big[\tr(Z^2) + \tr(ZZ^\top)\big]
\;\le\; \tr(ZZ^\top) = \tr(SB^\top B S^\top) = \tr(SKS^\top),
\]
because $\tr(ZZ^\top)-\tr(Z^2) = \tfrac12\lVert
Z-Z^\top\rVert_F^2\ge0$. The converse thus uses \emph{two}
inequalities, both machine-checked: $\tr(XX^\top)\ge0$ (Lean:
\texttt{trace\_mul\_transpose\_self\_nonneg}, applied to symmetric
$X = P-Q$ and to $Z - Z^\top$) and $\tr(Z^2)\le\tr(ZZ^\top)$ (Lean:
\texttt{trace\_sq\_le\_trace\_mul\_transpose}). Hence cost
$\ge \tr(SS^\top) - \tr(SKS^\top) = \tr(S(I-K)S^\top)$.

\emph{Achievability.} For $F^\star = SK$, $\Sigma_w^\star =
SK(I-K)S^\top$ (PSD since $\operatorname{spec}K\subset[0,1]$):
$N^\star = SK^2S^\top + SK(I-K)S^\top = SKS^\top$ by the ring
identity $K^2 + K(I-K) = K$ \emph{(Lean: \texttt{revelation\_key},
\texttt{revelation\_variance} --- corrected from v0.2's erroneous
citation of the telescope identity, R-IND-5 finding 11)}; the cost
telescopes to $\tr(S(I-K)S^\top)$ by $(I-K)^2 + K(I-K) = I-K$
\emph{(Lean: \texttt{shrink\_dither\_key},
\texttt{shrink\_dither\_cost})}. $K$-verification (display corrected
from v0.2, R-IND-5 finding 4): with $S = U\Sigma V^\top$ (thin SVD;
$V$ is $n\times n$ orthogonal by full column rank, $U$ has
orthonormal columns) and $K\succ0$,
\[
N^\star = U(\Sigma V^\top K V \Sigma)U^\top,\qquad
(N^\star)^{+} = U(\Sigma V^\top K V \Sigma)^{-1}U^\top,
\]
(the four Moore--Penrose conditions hold since $U$ has orthonormal
columns), whence
\[
F^{\star\top}(N^\star)^{+}F^\star
= KV\Sigma(\Sigma V^\top K V \Sigma)^{-1}\Sigma V^\top K
= KV(V^\top K V)^{-1}V^\top K = K,
\]
using $\Sigma(\Sigma B\Sigma)^{-1}\Sigma = B^{-1}$ with
$B = V^\top KV$ and $VV^\top = I$. Singular $K$: exact attainment
holds directly, since with $B=V^\top KV\succeq0$ one has
$B\Sigma(\Sigma B\Sigma)^{+}\Sigma B=B$, so the pseudoinverse identity
gives $F^{\star\top}(N^\star)^{+}F^\star=K$. (Corrected 2026-11-20,
VI-30: v0.3 said the harness verifies singular and projection $K$; it
does not --- V1 draws $K\succ0$ at $m=n$. For $m>n$, $N^\star=SKS^\top$
is singular, outside the $N\succ0$ class of the definition, and the
attainment is in the limit sense.)

\emph{(iii)} $\KF{\cdot}$ is the support function of $\mathcal W_k$
(Ky Fan), giving the inner max; linear-plus-support-function is
convex; Sion/von Neumann gives the saddle.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Deception requires dither; v0.1 Conjecture 2
\textbf{refuted}]\label{cor:dither}
A noiseless record ($\Sigma_w=0$, $F$ full row rank) has $K =
F^\top(FF^\top)^{-1}F$ \emph{idempotent}: revelation is a
projection, all-or-nothing per direction --- rescaling a noiseless
record shields nothing (the reader renormalizes). Fractional
revelation --- which an optimum has exactly when it has a contested
coordinate (Theorem~\ref{thm:diagonal}; optima at a positive level with
nothing contested, and blackout optima, need none, and when $J'(0+)=0$ a
blackout optimum coexists with contested ones) --- is achievable \emph{only} with
$\Sigma_w\neq0$. Optimal deception is shrink-\emph{and}-dither:
randomization is intrinsic, not a discrete-support artifact.
\textbf{v0.1's Conjecture 2 asserted the opposite; it is refuted by
this corollary} --- the diagonal instances demand dither trace $0.19$
at the optimum. \textbf{The diagnosis of the v0.1 probe finding (C4,
``never dithers''), however, has itself been corrected twice, and
both corrections stay on the record.} v0.2 attributed C4 to an
instrument artifact ($\varepsilon I$ jitter supplying free noise).
The R-IND-5 pass refuted that mechanism quantitatively (the jitter
route books blackout-level cost at mid-range revelation --- finding
8), and the V10 measurement settles it: at the v0.1 probe's own
$(\lambda,k)$ cells, the exact optima are \emph{near-projections}
(fractional mass at the solver's resolution, about $10^{-13}$ by
subgradient clipping --- the solver's demonstrated accuracy, V9a, is
about $7\times10^{-3}$ --- and required dither $\le 2\times
10^{-12}$ at three of four cells; $0.11$ and $0.027$ at the fourth).
The v0.1 instances needed $\approx$no dither; they were
\emph{unrepresentative}, not mismeasured. (v0.3 went further and said
that generic non-commuting instances optimize at near-projections;
that is contradicted by V9b's own random general instance, whose $K$
has fractional eigenvalue $0.270$ at the development seed, and $0.461$
and $0.060$ at the governed seed,
and by the S1 scan, and is withdrawn, VI-30.) The v0.1 poker-facing reading
still inverts as v0.2 said: mixing in discrete games is the shadow
of the dither requirement, now confirmed by the twin probe
(\S\ref{sec:twin}).
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}[No commitment gap at LQG scope; v0.1 Conjecture 4
resolved]\label{cor:commit}
By Theorem~\ref{thm:reduction}(iii), minimax $=$ maximin \emph{for
the reduced game in which the reader's strategy is the whitened
attention operator $W$} --- a reader who may condition its read on
the encoder's policy $(F,\Sigma_w)$ itself (the population/HUD
setting: the pool reads the record actually played). Conditioning on
$N$ alone is not enough: with $N=I$, $M=I$, $c=0.8$, the policies
$F_1=c\,e_1e_1^\top$ and $F_2=c\,e_1e_2^\top$ (each with
$\Sigma_w=I-FF^\top$) share $N$, yet $W=e_1e_1^\top$ pays $0.64$
against one and $0$ against the other (corrected 2026-11-20, VI-30).
In that game, encoder commitment is worthless. \textbf{Scope correction
(R-IND-5 finding 9):} this does \emph{not} establish the claim for
the concrete game in which the reader commits to a mixture over
fixed instruments $\Theta$ \emph{without} knowledge of $N$: the map
from $\Theta$ to attention, $P_U = N^{1/2}\Theta^\top(\Theta
N\Theta^\top)^{-1}\Theta N^{1/2}$, depends on the encoder's $N$, so
a committed $\Theta$-mixture cannot enforce a fixed $W^\ast$ across
encoder deviations. Whether a commitment gap exists in the
fixed-instrument game is settled at commuting scope for $k=1$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:nogap}) and \textbf{open} otherwise. What stands:
where no saddle attention is a projection (some eigenvalue lies in $(0,1)$), the reader must mix
(a projection --- an extreme point of $\mathcal W_k$ --- announced
first is exploitable); at a positive level with nothing contested a projection can be
maximin (at the witness of Theorem~\ref{thm:diagonal}'s integral
example, $W^\ast=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,0)$ attains the value). The
poker structure is recovered, not assumed, in the policy-conditioned
reading.
\end{corollary}

\section{The partition/tie theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Partition and ties, general scope]
\label{thm:partition}
Let $(K^\ast,W^\ast)$ be any saddle point of $\mathcal L$, and set
$G^\ast = M^{1/2}K^\ast M^{1/2}$,
$\Delta^\ast = \lambda M^{1/2}W^\ast M^{1/2} - \Sigma_S$. Then:
\begin{itemize}
\item[(i)] \emph{(Pricing.)} $K^\ast$ minimizes the \emph{linear}
functional $\tr(K\Delta^\ast)$ over $0\preceq K\preceq I$: on the
negative eigenspace of $\Delta^\ast$, $K^\ast = I$ (\textbf{revealed}
--- the conceded directions, where even full attention is priced
below value, together with the submerged directions the reader
ignores); on the positive eigenspace, $K^\ast = 0$
(\textbf{blacked out}); every eigenvector of $K^\ast$ with
fractional eigenvalue lies in $\ker\Delta^\ast$
(\textbf{contested}): on contested directions the reader's mixed
attention prices revelation to exact encoder indifference,
$\lambda\, M^{1/2}W^\ast M^{1/2} = \Sigma_S$.
\item[(ii)] \emph{(Attention.)} $W^\ast$ maximizes $\tr(WG^\ast)$
over $\mathcal W_k$: $W^\ast = P_{>} + Z$ with $P_{>}$ the projector
onto eigenvalues $>\lambda_k(G^\ast)$ and $Z$ supported on the
$\lambda_k(G^\ast)$-eigenspace.
\item[(iii)] \emph{(Tie.)} If $W^\ast$ has an eigenvalue in $(0,1)$
--- the reader mixes --- then
$\lambda_k(G^\ast) = \lambda_{k+1}(G^\ast)$.
\end{itemize}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
(i) and (ii) are the saddle conditions; both subproblems are linear
over spectral polytopes, so optimizers are as described (for (ii),
Ky Fan's maximum principle with its equality cases). (iii): suppose
$\lambda_k > \lambda_{k+1}$. The eigenvalue group of value
$\lambda_k$ then ends at position $k$; maximizers put weight $1$ on
all eigendirections with value $>\lambda_k$ (count $k-r$, where $r$
is the multiplicity of $\lambda_k$) and the remaining budget $r$
exactly fills the group --- forcing $W^\ast = P_{\ge}$, a
projection. Contrapositive gives the claim.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Diagonal closed form: the attention water level]
\label{thm:diagonal}
Let $\Sigma_S = \operatorname{diag}(s_1^2,\dots,s_n^2)\succ0$,
$M = \operatorname{diag}(\mu_1,\dots,\mu_n)$, $1\le k<n$, $\lambda>0$,
under genericity: (G1) $\mu_i$ distinct, positive; (G2) $\lambda\mu_i \neq
s_i^2$ for all $i$. Call $i$ \emph{conceded} ($i\in C$) if
$\lambda\mu_i<s_i^2$ and \emph{shieldable} ($i\in S$) if
$\lambda\mu_i>s_i^2$, and let
\[
J(t)=\lambda kt+\sum_{i\in C}\lambda(\mu_i-t)_+ +\sum_{i\in S}s_i^2\Bigl(1-\frac t{\mu_i}\Bigr)_+,
\qquad t\ge0,
\]
convex and piecewise linear, with breakpoints $\{\mu_i\}$ and the end
$0$ of its domain.
Then a diagonal optimum $K^\ast=\operatorname{diag}(\rho_i)$ of the
reduced program exists, and the diagonal optima are exactly
$\rho_i=1$ ($i\in C$), $\rho_i=\min(1,t^\ast/\mu_i)$ ($i\in S$), for
$t^\ast$ a minimizer of $J$; the minimizers form a closed interval whose
endpoints lie in $\{0\}\cup\{\mu_i\}$, and the diagonal optimum is unique
iff $\rho(t)$ is constant on it, that is, iff the interval is a point or
no $i\in S$ has $\mu_i$ above its left endpoint. The \textbf{water level} $t^\ast$ falls in
one of three regimes: the \textbf{blackout regime} $t^\ast=0$, which is
optimal iff $J'(0+)=\lambda(k-|C|)-\sum_{i\in S}s_i^2/\mu_i\ge0$; or a
positive level $t^\ast>0$ with the contested set nonempty or empty.
(Corrected 2026-11-20: v0.2--v0.5 stated only the two positive-level
regimes, with $t^\ast$ at a breakpoint $\mu_j$; the blackout regime was
missed, and the v0.5 statement is false on an open set --- VI-30,
NEG-17; witness $\mu=(5,3,2,1)$, $s^2=(10,0.1,0.1,0.1)$, $\lambda=1$,
$k=2$, where $J(0)=5.3<J(\mu_j)$.) With $g_i := \mu_i\rho_i$:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Conceded} ($\lambda\mu_i < s_i^2$, $\mu_i > t^\ast$):
$\rho_i = 1$, $g_i = \mu_i$, attention $\theta_i = 1$. (The lazy
regime: shielding costs more than the priced leak.)
\item \textbf{Contested} ($\lambda\mu_i > s_i^2$, $\mu_i > t^\ast>0$):
$\rho_i = t^\ast/\mu_i\in(0,1)$, $g_i = t^\ast$ exactly, and
$\theta_i = s_i^2/(\lambda\mu_i)\in(0,1)$: \emph{the reader's mixing
frequency equals the ratio of shielding cost to priced attention ---
pinned by encoder indifference.}
\item \textbf{Level} ($\mu_j = t^\ast>0$): $\rho_j = 1$, absorbing the
attention slack $\theta_j = k - \#\{i\in C:\mu_i>t^\ast\} -
\sum_{\text{contested}} s_i^2/(\lambda\mu_i)=J'(\mu_j+)/\lambda\in
[0,\min(1, s_j^2/(\lambda\mu_j))]$; it is interior unless $t^\ast$ ends
a flat stretch of $J$, where it can be $0$ or the cap (e.g.\ $\theta_j=0$
at $\mu=(4,2,1)$, $s^2=(2,1,0.5)$, $\lambda=1$, $k=1$, $t^\ast=1$, with
two coordinates contested).
\item \textbf{Submerged} ($\mu_i < t^\ast$): $\rho_i = 1$, $g_i =
\mu_i$, $\theta_i = 0$: fully revealed, unread --- free exploitation
by budget exhaustion rather than by structural invisibility.
\item \textbf{Blacked out} (only when $t^\ast=0$; every $i\in S$):
$\rho_i=0$, $g_i=0$, attention $\theta_i\in[s_i^2/(\lambda\mu_i),1]$
with $\sum_{i\in S}\theta_i=k-|C|$: fully shielded at a level where
the reader's leftover attention is worth less than the shielding.
\end{itemize}
In the blackout regime $|C|<k$ and $S\neq\emptyset$, no coordinate is
contested (contested needs $t^\ast>0$), the conceded ones are read in
full, and no saddle $W^\ast$ is a projection: its diagonal is $1$ on
$C$, and a matrix $0\preceq W\preceq I$ with a unit diagonal entry has
no other entry in that row, so $W^\ast=I_C\oplus W^\ast_{SS}$; the
$S$-block is $\succeq\operatorname{diag}(s_i^2/(\lambda\mu_i))\succ0$
with trace $k-|C|<|S|$, so it has an eigenvalue in $(0,1)$. Moreover the \textbf{tie}
$\lambda_k(G^\ast) = \lambda_{k+1}(G^\ast) = t^\ast$ holds \emph{iff}
$t^\ast=0$ or the contested set is nonempty, that is, iff some
coordinate is contested or blacked out. At a positive level with the
contested set empty every $\theta_i\in\{0,1\}$ and the top-$k$
spectrum is strict under (G1) (e.g.\ $\mu = (4, 2.5, 1.8)$,
$s^2 = (5,5,5)$, $\lambda = 1$, $k = 2$: all conceded,
$\theta = (1,1,0)$).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\emph{Diagonality (symmetrization).} The reduced program is convex
(Theorem~\ref{thm:reduction}(iii)) and invariant under $K\mapsto
DKD$ for every sign matrix $D = \operatorname{diag}(\pm1)$ (both
$\Sigma_S$ and $M$ commute with $D$; the spectrum of
$M^{1/2}DKDM^{1/2} = D M^{1/2}KM^{1/2}D$ is unchanged). Averaging
$K$ over the $2^n$ sign conjugations preserves feasibility, does not
increase the convex objective, and returns
$\operatorname{diag}(K)$; a diagonal optimum exists.

\emph{Per-coordinate cost.} At diagonal $K$, coordinate $i$ carries
SNR $\rho_i$ at cost $s_i^2(1-\rho_i)$ --- the scalar case of
Theorem~\ref{thm:reduction}(ii), by the exact completion of squares
$(f-s)^2 + f^2(1-\rho)/\rho = s^2(1-\rho) + (f-\rho s)^2/\rho$
\emph{(Lean: \texttt{scalar\_shielding\_identity},
\texttt{scalar\_shielding\_cost\_bound})}, minimized at the
shrink-and-dither point $f=\rho s$, $w = \rho(1-\rho)s^2$.

\emph{Water level.} With $\phi_k(g) = \min_{t\in\mathbb R}\,[kt +
\sum_i(g_i-t)_+]$: for $g\ge0$ and $k<n$ the minimum is attained at
some $t\ge0$, since for $t<0$ the bracket is $\sum_ig_i+(k-n)t$, which
exceeds its value $\sum_ig_i$ at $t=0$. The program is jointly convex
in $(\rho,t)$ on $[0,1]^n\times[0,\infty)$ and separable at fixed $t$:
coordinate $i$'s objective $s_i^2(1-\rho_i) + \lambda(\mu_i\rho_i -
t)_+$ has constant slopes $-s_i^2 + \lambda\mu_i$ above the kink and
$-s_i^2 < 0$ below, so by (G2) its unique minimizer is the kink
$\rho_i = \min(1, t/\mu_i)$ for $i\in S$ and $\rho_i = 1$ for $i\in C$:
linear-versus-linear forces bang-bang-to-the-kink --- this is where
the tie mechanism lives. Minimizing over $\rho$ at fixed $t$ gives
$J(t)$, a sum of convex piecewise-linear functions of $t$ with kinks at
the $\mu_i$, on the half-line $t\ge0$. Its minimizers form a closed
interval whose endpoints are kinks or the end point $0$. For an optimal
$\rho$, a $t$ attaining $\phi_k(\mu\circ\rho)$ makes $(\rho,t)$ jointly
optimal, so $t$ minimizes $J$ and $\rho$ is the unique minimizer at
that $t$; the diagonal optima are therefore exactly those stated. By
convexity $t^\ast=0$ is a minimizer iff
$J'(0+)=\lambda k-\lambda|C|-\sum_{i\in S}s_i^2/\mu_i\ge0$. The
reader's attention is a subgradient of $\phi_k$: $\theta_i=1$ where
$g_i>t^\ast$, $\theta_i=0$ where $g_i<t^\ast$, $\theta_i\in[0,1]$ where
$g_i=t^\ast$, with $\sum_i\theta_i=k$; the encoder's first-order
conditions fix the rest --- $\theta_i=s_i^2/(\lambda\mu_i)$ at an
interior kink (contested), the cap $\theta_j\le s_j^2/(\lambda\mu_j)$ at
a level coordinate $\rho_j=1$, and $\theta_i\ge s_i^2/(\lambda\mu_i)$
at a blacked-out $\rho_i=0$, where only increasing $\rho_i$ is feasible.

\emph{Tie.} \emph{Blackout} ($t^\ast=0$): $G^\ast$ has the $|C|$ values
$\mu_i$, $i\in C$, and zeros on $S$. If $S=\emptyset$ then
$J'(0+)=\lambda(k-n)<0$, so $S\neq\emptyset$; then
$J'(0+)\ge0$ gives $\lambda(k-|C|)\ge\sum_{i\in S}s_i^2/\mu_i>0$, so
$|C|<k$. With $k<n=|C|+|S|$, both $\lambda_k$ and $\lambda_{k+1}$ are
zeros: the tie holds at $0$. Each $\theta_i$, $i\in S$, is at least
$s_i^2/(\lambda\mu_i)>0$, and they sum to the integer $k-|C|<|S|$, so at
least one is fractional. \emph{Positive level, contested
$=\{i_1,\dots,i_c\}$, $c\ge1$:} the spectrum of $G^\ast$ has the values
above $t^\ast$ (count $a$), then the tied group $\{t^\ast\}$, then the
submerged values. The tied group is the contested coordinates, plus
the level coordinate $j$ when $t^\ast=\mu_j$; it has multiplicity
$c+\ell$ with $\ell\in\{0,1\}$ ($\ell=0$ only at a $t^\ast$ inside a
flat stretch of $J$). The budget gives $k = a +
\sum_{\text{contested}}\theta_i + \ell\theta_j$ with every contested
$\theta_i\in(0,1)$, so $a < k < a + c + \ell$ (which rules out $c=1$
when $\ell=0$), hence both $\lambda_k$ and $\lambda_{k+1}$ lie in the
tied group: $\lambda_k = \lambda_{k+1} = t^\ast$. \emph{Positive level,
contested $=\emptyset$:} every $\rho_i=1$, so the spectrum is
$\{\mu_i\}$, distinct by (G1), and the budget $k=a+\ell\theta_j$ with
$a$ and $k$ integers forces $\theta_j\in\{0,1\}$: strict, integral.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The poker reading]
On contested directions (a) the reader mixes (fractional $\theta$ =
randomized attention --- bluff-catching frequencies), (b) the encoder
part-shields (fractional $\rho$ = balanced ranges), and (c) given the
level $t^\ast$, each side's frequency is pinned by the \emph{other's}
indifference
($\theta_i = s_i^2/(\lambda\mu_i)$; $g_i = t^\ast$), as in poker. They
are not the whole mixing support (corrected 2026-11-20, VI-30, and
2026-11-21, VI-35): the reader can also mix on a level coordinate, fully
revealed, with $\theta_j=J'(\mu_j+)/\lambda$, which is $0$ or the cap at
the ends of a flat stretch (so in the integral regime it need not mix),
and on blacked-out coordinates, fully shielded, where $\theta_i$ is
bounded below by $s_i^2/(\lambda\mu_i)$ and pinned only when
$J'(0+)=0$; the encoder's contested $\rho_i=t^\ast/\mu_i$ is pinned
given $t^\ast$ but not unique on a contested flat stretch; and the
spectrum ties in the blackout regime with nothing contested. Conceded directions are ``too cheap to balance''
(the lazy regime the probe found); submerged directions are
exploitation for free by budget exhaustion; $\ker\Theta$ (fixed
reads) was v0.1's structural free-exploitation and survives as the
$W$-null space.
\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[The binary water-level theorem]\label{thm:binary}
In the binary disclosure game of \S\ref{sec:twin} with per-spot
policy $(q_0, q_1)$:
\emph{(i) (Exact channel algebra.)} The resolved-variance ratio has
the closed form
\[
\rho(q_0, q_1) \;=\;
\frac{(q_1 - q_0)^2}{(q_0 + q_1)\,(2 - q_0 - q_1)}
\qquad\text{(Lean: \texttt{rho\_closed\_form}),}
\]
on the nondegenerate domain, with the convention $\rho = 0$ at the
constant channels.
\emph{(ii) (The frontier, as a theorem.)} Since $u(2-u)\le1$ for
$u = q_0+q_1$, every policy satisfies
$\mathrm{err} \ge (1-\sqrt\rho)/2$, with equality iff $q_1\ge q_0$
and ($q_0+q_1=1$ or $q_0=q_1$): with $v=q_1-q_0$, $\mathrm{err}=(1-v)/2$
and $v^2=\rho\,u(2-u)$, so equality is $v=\sqrt\rho$, that is $v\ge0$
and ($u=1$ or $v=0$). (Corrected 2026-11-20, VI-31: v0.5 said
``exactly on the symmetric family $q_0+q_1=1$'', which fails both ways,
e.g.\ at $(0.8,0.2)$ and at $(0.3,0.3)$.) Lean: \texttt{frontier\_bound};
\texttt{frontier\_attained} proves the ``if'' half on the $u=1$
branch; \texttt{symmetric\_rho}. Deterministic
policies have $\rho\in\{0,1\}$. The 091 pilot's SLSQP refinement is
hereby superseded by proof. The frontier cost
$c(\rho) = s^2(1-\sqrt\rho)/2$ is convex (midpoint concavity of
$\sqrt{\cdot}$, Lean: \texttt{sqrt\_midpoint\_concave}, plus
continuity).
\emph{(iii) (Saddle and classes.)} The reduced game
$\sum_i c_i(\rho_i) + \lambda\,\phi_k(\mu\circ\rho)$ is convex; a
saddle against the attention polytope
$\{\theta\in[0,1]^n,\ \sum\theta = k\}$ exists (Sion). At a saddle,
each coordinate's response to $\theta_i$ is governed by the exact
completion of squares
$s^2(1-x)/2 + cx^2 = s^2/2 - s^4/(16c) + c\,(x - s^2/(4c))^2$
($x=\sqrt\rho$, $c = \lambda\theta_i\mu_i$; Lean:
\texttt{shield\_foc\_identity}, \texttt{\_bound}): at fixed attention
the best response is $x_i=\min(1,s_i^2/(4\lambda\theta_i\mu_i))$. The
five classes measured by 091 --- conceded, interior-contested-above,
tied (fractional $\theta_i = s_i^2/(4\lambda\sqrt{\mu_i t^\ast})$),
level, submerged --- describe the saddles found there; they are
\emph{not derived here in general}. In particular the reader's water
level $t^\ast$ need not be unique (in 091's P3 any $t\in[1,2.25]$ is
optimal, and coordinate 2 is level or submerged according to the
choice); the attention budget $D(t)=\sum_i\theta_i(t)$, the total
attention the coordinates' first-order conditions call for at level
$t$, jumps at each $t=\mu_i$, so the
budget condition reads $D(t^\ast+)\le k\le D(t^\ast-)$ rather than
$D(t^\ast)=k$, and the level coordinate then takes the slack, with
$\theta_j\in[0,\min(1,s_j^2/(4\lambda\mu_j))]$; and conceding needs
$\mu_i>t^\ast$ as well as $s_i^2\ge4\lambda\mu_i$. (Scoped 2026-11-20,
VI-31.)
\emph{(iv) (Ties.)} Any optimal attention vector satisfies the
exchange property --- it cannot be unsaturated ($\theta_i<1$) at a
strictly leakier coordinate while positive at a less leaky one (Lean:
\texttt{attention\_exchange}) --- so any two fractional coordinates
carry \emph{equal} $g=:\gamma$ (Lean: \texttt{fractional\_pair\_tie}),
coordinates with $\theta_i=1$ have $g_i\ge\gamma$, and those with
$\theta_i=0$ have $g_i\le\gamma$. Since $k$ is an integer, a lone
fractional coordinate is impossible ($\sum\theta$ would not be an
integer); with $a$ saturated and $f\ge2$ fractional coordinates,
$\#\{g>\gamma\}\le a<k<a+f\le\#\{g\ge\gamma\}$, so fractional attention
forces a genuine tie $\lambda_k = \lambda_{k+1}$. The tie is
reader-side and alphabet-independent, exactly as \S\ref{sec:twin}
measured.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[No fixed-instrument commitment gap at commuting

\theta^\ast)$ be a saddle of Theorem~\ref{thm:diagonal} with value
$V^\ast$, and let the reader commit, before seeing anything, to
glances along the eigenbasis directions $u_i=Sq_i/s_i$ in record space
($q_i$ the joint eigenvectors of $\Sigma_S$ and $M$).
\emph{(a) $k=1$.} If the reader commits to one glance, at direction
$u_i$ with probability $\theta^\ast_i$ --- the fixed rank-one-instrument
game over all mixtures of reads --- the encoder's infimum over ALL
policies $(F,\Sigma_w)$, including non-diagonal ones, equals $V^\ast$:
\textbf{for one glance the v0.3 open question is resolved --- there is
no commitment gap at commuting scope, and the optimal committed mixture
is explicit}.
\emph{(b) $k\ge2$.} The same holds for the \emph{additive axis-glance
surrogate}, in which each glance $u_i$ is read separately and the
leakage is $\sum_i\theta^\ast_iR(u_i)$, the reader's strategies being
axis weights in the attention polytope $\{\theta\in[0,1]^n,\sum\theta=k\}$.
This is not the rank-$k$ joint-subset game of Corollary~\ref{cor:commit}
(a committed mixture of $k$-subsets read jointly), whose converse is
open; a reader free to choose non-axis glances read separately would be
stronger still (against the 6-coordinate saddle policy, glances
$(e_1\pm e_2)/\sqrt2$ yield $4.88$, against a top-2 value of $3.50$, VI-31).
(Scope corrected 2026-11-20, VI-31: v0.5 stated the resolution for all
$k$, and for ``its binary twin'', which the LQG proof does not cover.)
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
($\le$): evaluate the saddle policy against the committed mixture.
At the saddle every glance resolves its coordinate's saddle leakage,
so the payoff is exactly $V^\ast$ against a mixture with marginals
$\theta^\ast$ (and at most $V^\ast$ against other mixtures). ($\ge$): against axis
glances the objective \emph{separates across record rows} --- the
glance at row $i$ sees only $N_{ii} = \lVert f_i\rVert^2 + w_{ii}$
($f_i$ the $i$-th row of $F$), the value loss and $\tr\Sigma_w$ are
row/diagonal sums, and off-diagonal $\Sigma_w$ entries appear
nowhere. Within a row, moving cross-signal mass into dither
($f_{ij}\mapsto 0$, $w_{ii}\mapsto w_{ii} + f_{ij}^2$, $j\neq i$)
leaves the value cost and the read variance unchanged and weakly
lowers the resolved numerator (Lean: \texttt{junk\_domination}), so
scalar rows are optimal; the scalar row problem reduces to the
shrink-and-dither cost (Lean: \texttt{scalar\_shielding\_identity},
\texttt{\_cost\_bound}), giving row value $\ge \min_\rho\,
[s_i^2(1-\rho) + \lambda\theta^\ast_i\mu_i\rho]$; summing and using
the saddle property of $(\rho^\ast,\theta^\ast)$ gives $\ge
V^\ast$; at $k=1$ the single glance is exactly this additive model.
\emph{Falsification attempt, not a certificate:} S2 of
\texttt{go16\_theory\_extensions.py} searches encoder policies by local
optimization from many starts, including far random ones, and FAILS if
any start reaches below $V^\ast-10^{-8}$; a search can only fail to
find a counterexample to ($\ge$), not prove it. (v0.5 reported a
``gap'' of $1.4\times10^{-12}$ from a gate that checked only
$\inf\le V^\ast$, which certifies nothing about ($\ge$), and a
$-1.4\times10^{-10}$ from a development-seed run; corrected 2026-11-20,
VI-31.)
\emph{Open residue:} the rank-$k$ \emph{joint-subset} instrument
variant is upper-bounded by $V^\ast$, but its converse needs an
inequality not given here --- the natural one, that a $k$-subset read
jointly leaks at least the sum of its rows read singly, fails for
redundant rows (a fresh-context search found no gap on two instances,
VI-31; evidence, not proof); and the non-commuting fixed-instrument game
remains open.
\end{proof}

\textbf{The commuting-alignment scan (S1, exploratory).} The V10

Conjecture~\ref{conj:converse} is updated accordingly: the refined

\end{conjecture}
