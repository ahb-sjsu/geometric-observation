To the Editor-in-Chief and Associate Editor\\
IEEE Transactions on Information Theory

Dear Editor:

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, ``Rate--Conditional-Entropy
Tradeoffs with Encoder-Observed Context,'' for publication in the IEEE
Transactions on Information Theory. I am the sole author. The review
manuscript is in single-column format and is within the page limit for
review; a two-column trial build runs to 23 pages.

The paper evaluates Steinberg's common-reconstruction functional on an
augmented Gaussian source: the encoder observes a pair $(Y,V)$, the
distortion binds $Y$ alone, and a third party holds a noisy copy of the
context $V$ that no decoder uses. Two results are primary: a closed form
for the minimal conditional content, the larger root of an explicit
quadratic, for $Y$ and $V$ scalar linear functionals of an
arbitrary-dimensional jointly Gaussian source; and the exact region of
jointly achievable rate and conditional content, with a two-water-level
Pareto frontier and a strict tradeoff exactly when the described variable
and the context are partially correlated, persisting at the clean-context
boundary. A structural consequence is non-determination: the function is
not a functional of the reduced source--side-information marginal, so the
encoder's access to the context changes the operational problem.
Extensions give the frontier with vector context, the binary
rate--content frontier through a tilt equation, and a direct Gaussian
operational theorem whose achievability quantizes the conditioner, the
source, and the reproduction and invokes the finite-alphabet theorem. Consistency anchors
recover the classical, Gray, and Steinberg corners.

Two earlier documents are relevant, and they are distinct from one
another. First, this Transactions declined a different manuscript of
mine, IT-26-0996, on 27 July 2026 without review, on scope and
presentation; it was a broad synthesis. I ask the editors to read the
present submission independently of it: this is a single-object work in
the classical rate--distortion genre and shares no theorem with that
declined submission. The terminology concerns raised in that review have
been addressed here by adopting standard source-coding vocabulary
throughout.

Second, and separately, the manuscript cites an archived preliminary
manuscript by the author, reference [36] of the submission (Zenodo record,
doi:10.5281/zenodo.21776291), as disclosed in its Related Work section.
That record is not a prior publication of this paper, and the two are
distinguished as follows. Inherited from [36], with attribution: the
discrete operational region theorem and its single-letterization, and the
scalar Gaussian corner. New in the present manuscript, and not in [36]:
the closed form $L(D)=\tfrac12\log_2 g^\star$ with its achieving channel
for $Y$ and $V$ scalar linear functionals of an arbitrary-dimensional
Gaussian source (Theorem~14(a)), the exact rate--content region with its
two-noise-fraction Pareto frontier (Theorem~14(b)), the misalignment and
non-determination corollaries (Corollaries~19 and~21), the vector-context
extension, the binary rate--content frontier, the direct Gaussian
operational theorem (Appendix~A), and the Lu--Xu comparison (Appendix~C). Two components have
close predecessors that are credited in place: the pair-sufficiency
reduction is a distortion-preserving specialization of Armstrong's
information-bottleneck sufficiency, and the binary conditional-content
function coincides with the common-reconstruction rate of Lu et al., the
contribution there being the joint frontier. The present paper supersedes
the source-coding content of [36], which remains an archived evidence
artifact. The present manuscript's own sources, verification scripts, and
reproduction notebook are archived separately (doi:10.5281/zenodo.22678341)
and at a tagged path of the public repository named in the title
footnote.

The closed forms have been checked numerically against direct
minimization; the check scripts and an executed reproduction notebook are
submitted as supplementary material. The use of AI tools in preparing the
manuscript is disclosed in its Acknowledgment, as IEEE policy requires.

This manuscript is not under review elsewhere, and I have no conflicts of
interest to declare.
